# Team Hashes: AI Builder Cup 2026

**Core project: SafeShelf Global** (theme: Retail & Commerce)

> SafeShelf checks any product listing and answers two questions: **Is it safe?** (hazards spotted in buyer reviews and photos before an official recall, plus recall and certification checks) and **Is it real?** (copied photos, an impossible price, a brand-new website). All of this runs on one product-identity engine that recognises the same product under different brand names.

Deadline: **Oct 18, 2026, 11:59 PM IST** (we aim for Oct 17).
