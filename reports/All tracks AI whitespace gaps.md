# Build for the moment after incumbents stop

The strongest open problem across all six tracks is **a multilingual, multimodal "golden hour" scam-recovery copilot (BFSI)**. A scam victim uploads screenshots, payment receipts, chat exports and a voice note in Hindi, Tagalog, Thai or Japanese. Gemini rebuilds the timeline, classifies the scam, routes the case under the correct national regime (India 1930/NCRP, the Philippines' AFASA disputed-funds hold, Singapore's Shared Responsibility Framework, Australia's Scams Prevention Framework) and drafts the complaint and bank-dispute letter while the recovery window is still open. A bank-side console then clusters incoming reports by shared beneficiary account to surface mule accounts. This is the recommended #1 entry. Every funded scam tool found in the research works *before* a scam (ScamShield, Trend Micro, Google's Gemini Nano call detection). Recovery collapses from **25-40% within 24 hours to 5-10% after a week** ([Moneylife](https://moneylife.in/article/fraud-alert-use-golden-hour-to-report-scam-and-increase-your-chances-to-get-money-back/74091.html)). Southeast Asia alone lost an estimated **US$23.6B** to scams in 2024 ([Vietnam News on GASA](https://vietnamnews.vn/world/1725271/singapore-scam-victims-top-southeast-asia-losses-at-us-2-132-per-person-malaysia-second.html)). Close behind are a repositioned Manufacturing entry, and a real-time compliance whisperer for human live-commerce hosts in Retail. The Manufacturing entry has to be **"verify, don't author"**: it proves a migrant worker understood and can perform a procedure. The earlier idea of expert video in, multilingual SOP out is now a shipped product in Japan (Studist's Teachme AI). The same pattern holds across all six tracks. Incumbents and Google's own products own generation, chat and pre-event detection. The whitespace is the last mile *after* an event or *around* a platform's output: verification, recovery, official-form filling and human-in-the-loop assistance, for a specific JAPAC user under a regulation that took effect in 2025-26. Two logistics facts matter as much as the idea. Team changes close **Oct 11**, and **Gemini 2.5 retires on Vertex on Oct 16**, two days before the Oct 18 submission and in the middle of judging. Every build must target a newer model ID that the team has confirmed on day one.

## One pattern explains every track's whitespace

The research covered all six themes. BFSI, Retail, Future of Work and Sustainability were scanned in new notes. Manufacturing and Media come from the earlier deep-dive. One rule sorts crowded ideas from open ones. **Anything that generates, chats, summarises or detects before the fact is already sold**, often by the sponsor. Google ships Gemini Enterprise as an enterprise knowledge assistant and agent builder ([AI Business](https://aibusiness.com/agentic-ai/google-cloud-unveils-gemini-enterprise)). It ships Gemini Enterprise for Customer Experience for shopper discovery and cart agents ([IT Brief AU](https://itbrief.com.au/story/google-launches-gemini-cx-ai-to-unite-retail-journeys)), Flood Hub for riverine flood forecasting across 80 countries ([Google blog](https://blog.google/outreach-initiatives/sustainability/flood-hub-ai-flood-forecasting-more-countries/)), SKAI for satellite building-damage assessment ([UN Global Pulse](https://www.unglobalpulse.org/ai-from-google-research-and-un-boosts-humanitarian-disaster-response-wider-coverage-faster-damage-assessments)) and on-device scam-call detection ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/11/20/google-steps-up-ai-scam-protection-in-india-but-gaps-remain)). An entry that copies any of these competes with the judges' own flagship.

What remains open has three traits. The first is an **underserved JAPAC user**: a scam victim filing a report, a two-person compliance team at a rural bank, a migrant welder, a human livestream host, a barangay responder, an elderly evacuee. The second is a **dated regulatory "why now"**. Examples are the Philippines' AFASA rules (effective June 25, 2025), Australia's Scams Prevention Framework Act 2025, Vietnam's E-commerce Law 122/2025 (effective July 1, 2026), Indonesia's halal-label transition (ends Oct 17, 2026), Japan's 育成就労 system (FY2027), Japan's 2.7% disability quota (July 2026) and the EU's CBAM payment phase (from Jan 1, 2026). The third is **agentic, multimodal work that would break without GenAI**: reading messy phone evidence, reasoning over regulation, filling an official form and handing it to a human. That third trait is what the 40% Technical Merit and 25% Innovation weights reward.

Crowding by track is a stated expectation, not measured data: Future of Work and Sustainability are expected to be the most crowded, and Manufacturing the least. The ranking below applies a small explicit penalty to the crowded tracks and a small bonus to Manufacturing. Each team submits one solution under one theme ([HACKATHON.md rules](https://aibuildercup.com/)). Some ideas fit more than one theme, so the team should file each under the least crowded theme it honestly fits.

### Idea 1 — "Golden Hour" scam-recovery copilot (track pick, overall #1)

**Gap evidence.** The losses are large and widespread across the region. Thailand lost ฿115.3B ([GASA Thailand](https://gasa.org/knowledge-base/reports/state-of-scams-in-thailand-report-2025)). Taiwan lost US$2B, with 61% of adults victimised ([GASA Taiwan](https://gasa.org/knowledge-base/reports/state-of-scams-in-taiwan-report-2025)). The Philippines lost ₱280.5B ([Philstar](https://www.philstar.com/business/2025/11/26/2489831/pinoys-lose-p280-billion-scammers-report)). India's I4C logged about ₹19,813 crore lost, 2.18M cheating complaints and **32.4M calls to the 1930 helpline in 2025** ([Moneylife](https://moneylife.in/article/fraud-alert-india-is-losing-over-22000-crore-a-year-in-cyber-scams-and-the-worst-is-yet-to-come/80612.html)). Bank fraud models miss most of these cases because the victim authorised the transfer. A secondary source puts that share at 81.8% of Singapore cases; check it against SPF statistics before quoting it ([ai2.work](https://ai2.work/blog/singapore-s-mas-recruits-banks-and-ai-to-stop-scams-early)). Speed decides the outcome. Odisha recovered only **2.7%** of ₹370 crore ([The420](https://the420.in/?p=96456)). Uttar Pradesh reached **47%** with real-time bank-police coordination ([The420 UP](https://the420.in/uttar-pradesh-cyber-fraud-recovery-1930-cfmc-golden-hour/)). Regulators have just created the formal routes a victim needs to use. AFASA's implementing rules add a "temporary holding of disputed funds" process ([Context.ph](https://context.ph/2025/06/11/bsp-issues-implementing-rules-for-afasa-law/)). Australia's SPF Act obliges banks, telcos and platforms to "respond" and "report", with penalties up to A$50M ([Vixio](https://www.vixio.com/insights/pc-australia-enacts-world-leading-scams-prevention-framework)). Singapore's SRF assigns phishing losses to institutions that breach their duties ([Baker McKenzie](https://insightplus.bakermckenzie.com/bm/data-technology/singapore-mas-and-imda-seek-comments-on-proposed-shared-responsibility-framework-for-phishing-scams)).

**Nearest competitors and why they fall short.** ScamShield, Trend Micro and Gemini Nano all act before the transfer. Gemini Nano also runs only on Pixel 9 and only in English, and Pixel holds under 1% of India's phone market ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/11/20/google-steps-up-ai-scam-protection-in-india-but-gaps-remain)). After a scam, the victim gets human hotlines (1930, IDCARE, GASA). The research found **no product that turns a victim's raw evidence into a complete, jurisdiction-correct report and dispute packet**. That absence comes from a non-exhaustive search, so spend 30 minutes on day one searching SEA and Indian app stores for "scam recovery assistant" before committing.

**Buildability.** A Firebase PWA handles upload, voice and chat. A Cloud Run ADK agent does five things: Gemini multimodal extraction into a JSON schema (amounts, UTR/reference IDs, beneficiary accounts, scammer handles); scam-type classification (digital arrest, pig-butchering, fake police, phishing); function calls to per-country "playbooks" grounded by RAG over the AFASA, SPF Act, SRF and I4C documents; bilingual PDF generation; and a live golden-hour countdown and checklist. Firestore stores the cases. A bank-side console in BigQuery clusters reports by beneficiary account, which turns victim intake into mule intelligence for the small banks MuleHunter does not cover. An optional Gemma redaction pass strips PII before upload. That gives the privacy story and a real Gemma use.

**Demo data.** Synthetic scam screenshots and chat exports, PaySim-style transfers ([Kaggle PaySim](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/ealaxi/paysim1)), and IBM AMLSim to seed the mule graph ([GitHub](https://github.com/IBM/AMLSim)). Use public regulator PDFs as the grounding corpus.

**Judging fit.** It is strong on Technical Merit: multimodal plus multilingual plus tool calling plus grounded RAG plus graph analytics, and none of it works without GenAI. Impact is strong, with billions lost and dated regulation. Innovation comes from attacking the post-incident window that every incumbent ignores. UX matters because the user is distressed and the countdown and checklist design is visible. The main risks are legal-advice liability, which the team can manage by framing the tool as "drafts for you to submit" with a human sign-off, and untested speech accuracy in Tagalog and Thai.

### Idea 2 — Japan elder "pause-and-verify" companion (fold into Idea 1 as a mode)

Japan's "special fraud" losses roughly doubled to a record **¥142.3B** in 2025. Fake-police scams more than doubled to 11,014 cases and ¥100.5B, and people aged 65+ accounted for **59.2% of losses** ([Nippon.com](https://www.nippon.com/en/japan-data/h02795/fake-police-scams-double-in-japan-as-financial-losses-from-fraud-soar.html)). The current responses are blunt: police have proposed ATM limits for elderly people ([SCMP](https://scmp.com/week-asia/economics/article/3304007/japan-police-propose-atm-curbs-elderly-combat-fraud)), and the MIC issued a July 2026 request to strengthen countermeasures ([MIC](https://www.soumu.go.jp/main_sosiki/joho_tsusin/eng/pressrelease/2026/7/29_1.html)). The concept is a Gemini Live voice conversation in Japanese, triggered before a large transfer. It probes for known scripts ("the police said your account is being used for crime") and escalates to a family member it has nominated, with an explanation. The weakness is that the bank trigger has to be mocked, which narrows the demo. As a "prevention mode" inside the golden-hour copilot, it adds Japan coverage and a real-time voice showpiece at little extra cost.

### Idea 3 — Mule-investigation copilot for small and rural banks

MuleHunter covers only 23 banks. Co-operative banks took **234 of 304 RBI penalties** in 2024, according to a secondary source ([OnFinance AI](https://onfinanceainewsletter.beehiiv.com/p/rbi-s-56-32-crore-penalty-surge-how-ai-can-prevent-compliance-lapses-in-bfsi)). Indonesia's 1,468 remaining BPRs face forced consolidation under POJK 7/2024 ([Kontan](https://keuangan.kontan.co.id/news/ojk-percepat-konsolidasi-sejumlah-bpr-berguguran-sepanjang-2025)), and AFASA has made money muling a crime ([SunStar](https://www.sunstar.com.ph/cebu/bsp-unveils-rules-to-combat-financial-scams)). The build combines an AMLSim graph, Gemini-written network explanations, and STR or AFASA report drafts for a two-person compliance team. Its novelty comes from the target user rather than the technique. It sits close to AML vendors such as Tookitaki and Feedzai, so it works better as the bank-side half of Idea 1 than as a standalone entry.

### Idea 4 — Vernacular insurance "policy X-ray" and mis-selling detector (India)

IRDAI's FY25 report shows "unfair business practice" grievances up **14% to 26,667**, which is 22% of life-insurer complaints ([Insurance Business Asia](https://www.insurancebusinessmag.com/asia/news/breaking-news/irdai-sees-misselling-complaints-rise-amid-stable-grievances-561149.aspx)). The space is partly occupied. Insurance Samadhan is mostly a human-assisted service ([profile](https://valueforstartups.in/insurancesamadhan)), and Polifyx has a "Know Your Policy" feature ([Outlook Money](https://www.outlookmoney.com/amp/story/insurance/mis-selling-complaints-grew-112-since-2024-value-of-disputed-claims-rose-10-report)). What survives is a voice-first, regional-language check of **what the agent promised** (a WhatsApp voice note or chat) **against the policy wording**, run before the free-look window closes. That is a good multimodal fit with moderate innovation. A farmer-side crop-insurance claim advocate is also viable. It would cover PMFBY, where states have defaulted on about **₹6,450 crore** since FY20 ([IASGyan](https://www.iasgyan.in/daily-current-affairs/pradhan-mantri-fasal-bima-yojana-systemic-failures)). It fits Sustainability equally well, but the claim data is hard to obtain.

### Idea 1 — "LiveGuard": real-time compliance and Q&A whisperer for human live-commerce hosts (track pick)

**Gap evidence.** Video commerce is about **25% of SEA e-commerce GMV**, and more than **3M stores** now sell on video platforms ([Bain e-Conomy SEA 2025](https://www.bain.com/about/media-center/press-releases/sea/e-conomy-sea-2025/)). Vietnam's Law 122/2025, effective **July 1, 2026**, is the first to regulate livestream selling explicitly. It bars hosts from giving false or misleading information about a product's uses, origin, quality or price ([Vietnam News](https://vietnamnews.vn/economy/1725726/viet-nam-s-upcoming-e-commerce-law-to-tighten-rules-on-livestream-sellers.html)). China's Order 117 requires real-time monitoring and three-year record retention ([China Briefing](https://www.china-briefing.com/news/china-livestream-advertising-regulations-compliance/)). TikTok Shop now requires **real-time human speech** and penalises violations through its Creator Health Rating ([NetInfluencer](https://www.netinfluencer.com/tiktok-shop-bans-ai-voices-prerecorded-audio-from-promotional-livestreams/)). Compliance guidance already recommends "approved claims libraries, restricted language lists… real-time monitoring" ([Influencers-Time](https://www.influencers-time.com/tiktok-shop-supplement-livestream-compliance-framework/)).

**Nearest competitors and why they fall short.** AnyLive replaces the host, which TikTok now bans. Seller Assistant is a back-office chatbot. Platform moderation enforces after the fact. The research found **no seller-side tool that whispers "don't say 'cures acne' — not BPOM-registered" to a human host during the stream**.

**Buildability.** The Gemini Live API ingests the host's audio and video plus the chat feed over a Cloud Run websocket. RAG over Law 122, BPOM/halal rules and TikTok policy returns violations with the cited rule text. A teleprompter suggests compliant answers to viewer questions, which the human speaks aloud, so the tool stays within TikTok's rule. An audit log mirrors China's retention model. **This is the highest-risk build in the shortlist**, because real-time latency and false positives have to look good on camera. Pre-record the "live" session and replay it through the pipeline as a fallback.

**Demo data.** The team records its own mock livestream. The rules corpus comes from the Law 122 English text ([LuatVietnam](https://english.luatvietnam.vn/legal-updates/english-translation-of-the-2025-law-on-e-commerce-is-newly-published-892-106765-article.html)) and the halal circular ([SSEK](https://ssek.com/blog/indonesia-halal-certification-compliance-strengthened-mandatory-labelling-and-publication-for-certified-products/)).

**Judging fit.** It is the highest Innovation score in Retail: it assists the human instead of replacing them, which is the reverse of the crowded avatar trend. It is a natural real-time multimodal showcase for the 40% technical weight. Its Impact is slightly weaker than BFSI's, because no figures on livestream fines or enforcement were found.

### Idea 2 — Halal / BPOM label and listing checker from packaging photos (Indonesia first)

BPJPH Circular 7/2025 requires the official national halal label design and placement. It also requires certified products to publish their halal status on marketplaces and social media, and **the transition from the old MUI logo ends Oct 17, 2026** ([SSEK](https://ssek.com/blog/indonesia-halal-certification-compliance-strengthened-mandatory-labelling-and-publication-for-certified-products/); [Mondaq](https://www.mondaq.com/dodd-frank-consumer-protection-act/1703166/indonesia-halal-certification-compliance-strengthened-mandatory-labelling-and-publication-for-certified-products)). More than **80% of 700 ASEAN MSMEs** report cross-border e-commerce challenges ([TechRepublic](https://www.techrepublic.com/article/news-asean-cross-border-ecommerce-apac/)). Labelling rules differ by market, and re-labelling costs "fall most heavily on MSMEs" ([Alibaba Seller Blog](https://seller.alibaba.com/blogs/2026/southeast-asia/small-appliances/compliance-driven-market-reshuffle)). No tool was found that checks a pack photo plus a marketplace listing against the ID/MY/TH/VN rules and outputs fixes. Today, consultants and law firms do this work. The build is simple: Gemini vision reads the label, checks the halal logo version and BPOM number format, flags a missing digital halal publication, and rewrites the listing in Bahasa, Malay, Thai and Vietnamese. Gemma can run on-device for sellers who are offline. **Timing caveat:** the deadline passes the day before submission, so pitch the tool as being for the enforcement period that is starting, not for a deadline still to come. Scope it to food and cosmetics so that rule accuracy can be defended. It is technically lighter than LiveGuard. Expect about 4/5 on Tech and very low build risk.

### Idea 3 — Shopkeeper-side "store brain" for kirana, sari-sari and warung stores

India's roughly 13M kiranas saw their share fall from 95% to 92.6% between 2018 and 2023, and 46% of quick-commerce users cut kirana purchases ([Storyboard18](https://www.storyboard18.com/amp/how-it-works/quick-commerce-disrupts-indias-grocery-industry-kirana-stores-hit-hard-kearney-68991.htm)). Shelf recognition serves brands, while ledgers and B2B ordering are separate apps. The surviving angle is **agentic reconciliation for the shopkeeper**: a shelf photo becomes a list of stock gaps, which is matched against a distributor's WhatsApp price list (an image or PDF) to produce a draft order in the local language. Credit-aware khata hints and near-expiry markdowns can be folded in. SKU110K ([GitHub](https://github.com/eg4000/SKU110K_CVPR19)) and M5 ([Kaggle](https://www.kaggle.com/competitions/m5-forecasting-accuracy)) supply the demo data. It is buildable, but its novelty is moderate, and in the Philippines Packworks already narrows it.

### Honest re-assessment of the earlier top pick

The earlier report ranked "expert phone video → multilingual micro-SOP → novice check → voice quiz" first, with a 5/5 Innovation score. That score no longer holds. Teachme AI already **auto-generates multilingual step-by-step text and subtitled video manuals from video in Japan**, the exact market the earlier pitch targeted ([Notebookcheck](https://www.notebookcheck.net/Studist-launches-Teachme-AI-to-automate-multilingual-work-manual-creation-while-reducing-labor-by-over-80.863069.0.html)). Sensay already runs AI voice interviews to capture a departing employee's knowledge ([Barchart](https://www.barchart.com/story/news/36018738/sensay-launches-worlds-first-ai-offboarding-platform-for-knowledge-transfer)). A judge who knows the Japanese market would see a pitch that leads with "video in, multilingual SOP out" as a Teachme clone.

Three pieces of differentiation survive. None of them depends on the SOP-authoring step.

The first is **verification of performance**. The novice films an attempt, and Gemini compares it step by step with the expert video, with timestamped deviations. The only comparable work is MHI and Algomatic's GENIAC-PRIZE R&D on TIG welding, which is not a product ([MHI](https://www.mhi.com/news/26040801.html)). The notes found nothing showing that Teachme checks trainee attempts. That was not verified on Teachme's own feature pages, so check it on day one.

The second is **verification of comprehension with an audit trail**. A spoken quiz runs in Nepali, Burmese or Vietnamese and logs a per-worker pass/fail record for safety-critical procedures such as lockout. The Korean evidence is direct. Accidents among foreign workers "stem from an inability to read safety signs, operate emergency stop mechanisms or fully grasp verbal instructions" ([Korea Times](https://www.koreatimes.co.kr/southkorea/20260908/incheon-uses-game-show-format-in-safety-training-for-migrant-factory-workers)), and **18.7% of surveyed migrant shipyard workers were trained in a language they did not understand** ([BHRRC](https://www.business-humanrights.org/ar/أحدث-الأخبار/s-korea-report-highlights-safety-blind-spots-for-subcontracted-and-migrant-workers-in-shipyards)). Translation is what Teachme, Beekeeper and Augmentir sell. Proof of understanding is not.

The third is **a "why" interviewer for the kotsu (knack)**. It watches the veteran's video and asks timestamped follow-ups, such as "you paused here — what were you listening for?" Sensay interviews by voice only, in office settings. Teachme captures steps, not the reasoning behind them. This piece is the most exposed, because either vendor could add it quickly.

The repositioned entry is **"Genba Proof": capture once, then prove every worker understood and can perform the procedure**. It targets Korean and Japanese SMEs with migrant workforces. Japan has **2.571M foreign workers**, up 11.7% ([JREF citing MHLW](https://jref.com/threads/number-of-foreign-workers-in-japan-hits-record-2-57-million-in-2025.918430/)), and about 80% of manufacturers worry about skills transfer ([MONOist](https://monoist.itmedia.co.jp/mn/spv/2410/28/news002.html)). Its Innovation score falls from 5 to about 3.5, and its weighted score from 4.9 to about 4.5. It stays the best Manufacturing entry, and Manufacturing is expected to be the least crowded track. The build is unchanged from the earlier plan: ADK agents on Cloud Run for capture, novice comparison via structured JSON, Gemini TTS and Live quiz, and Firestore for records. Demo data comes from self-filmed clips (a bicycle chain change, a drill-press setup) plus hazard scenarios seeded from OSHA Severe Injury Reports ([OSHA](https://osha.gov/severe-injury-reports)). The main technical risk is still step alignment between two videos. Keep the clips short and controlled.

### Other Manufacturing whitespace

The **paper check-sheet digitiser with drift alerts** is the lowest-risk alternative. **54% of small and mid-size plants still run on pen, paper or spreadsheets** ([IoT Analytics](https://iot-analytics.com/mes-vendors-replace-pen-paper-spreadsheets/)), and MES vendors sell replacing paper rather than reading it. The demo can use handwritten sheets rendered from AI4I 2020 values ([UCI](https://archive.ics.uci.edu/dataset/601)). Generic IDP vendors were not ruled out. **Sensorless legacy-machine diagnosis from phone audio and video** beats the sensor incumbents on access, because Tractian targets firms with 100+ employees ([SoftwareConnect](https://softwareconnect.com/go/tractian-review-dddae)). It can use MIMII/DCASE audio ([Zenodo](https://zenodo.org/record/3384387)), but Gemini's accuracy on audio anomalies is unproven, so pitch it as triage. A **CBAM evidence pack built from messy multilingual SME paperwork** answers the CBAM payment phase. That phase hits 3,000-4,000 direct Indian steel and aluminium exporters, with default-value mark-ups rising to 30% by 2028 ([Mongabay India](https://india.mongabay.com/2026/06/european-union-border-tax-tests-indias-small-iron-and-steel-businesses/); [Business Today](https://www.businesstoday.in/amp/industry/story/eus-new-carbon-tax-could-hit-thousands-of-indian-msmes-can-they-adapt-536301-2026-06-11)). Free calculators already exist, so the open part is only the document-reading step. It fits Sustainability equally well but would be filed in the less crowded Manufacturing track.

### Idea 1 — Vernacular video claim triage for fact-checkers (track pick)

Social video news use rose from **52% to 65%** between 2020 and 2025. TikTok reaches **49% of Thais** for news ([Reuters DNR 2025](https://reutersinstitute.politics.ox.ac.uk/digital-news-report/2025/dnr-executive-summary)). Philippine fact-checkers say most AI tools are "programmed exclusively in English", and they reject full automation ([VERA Files](https://verafiles.org/articles/ph-fact-checkers-wary-of-ai-for-fact-checking)). That evidence is from 2023 and has no newer follow-up. In India, newsrooms can track Hindi, English or Bengali but not the long tail of regional languages ([Nieman Lab](https://www.niemanlab.org/2024/05/indian-journalists-are-the-frontline-against-election-deepfakes/)). Logically Facts covers four Indian languages with human teams ([Logically Facts](https://logicallyfacts.com/en/announcements/logically-facts-launches-fact-checking-in-four-indian-languages)). Gemini can ingest a whole Taglish or Hinglish TikTok, extract timestamped checkable claims, match them against ClaimReview data through the Fact Check Tools API, and draft a verdict for a human checker to approve. It scores well on Technical (native long video plus audio plus code-switching) and well on Innovation. One risk is unchecked: whether Google's own Fact Check Explorer already covers part of this.

### Idea 2 — AI audio description for Asian-language film, TV and local video

Blind audiences in South Asia have "almost no access" to audio description. Nobaflix's 500+ titles is the main counter-example ([Zero Project](https://zeroproject.org/view/project/23c0d84c-1e41-f011-8779-7c1e527683f1)). Gemini finds gaps in the dialogue and writes culturally aware descriptions that fit them, Cloud TTS voices them, and a describer review UI keeps a human in charge. Extending this to local YouTube and news clips differentiates it from Nobaflix's curated catalogue. It is a strong UX and accessibility story and is likely to compete for the Most Impactful and Best UI/UX prizes.

### Idea 3 — Yasashii Nihongo / easy-read conversion of news and alerts

Research on plain-Japanese generation is still active in 2026, which shows the problem is unsolved ([IEICE](https://ken.ieice.org/ken/paper/20260704BcvY/eng/)). It is technically light on its own. It belongs to the cross-track foreign-resident cluster described below, and it works better as a component than as a standalone entry. Cultural and regulatory localisation QA for ads across ID/MY/TH/VN/JP demos well, but no third-party evidence of demand was found.

### Idea 1 — "Yasashii Bridge": a workplace language bridge that teaches Japanese instead of replacing it

Japan's **2.571M foreign workers** ([VOV](https://kynguyenvuonminh.vov.vn/en/society/japan-sees-record-number-of-foreign-workers-vietnamese-top-the-list-post1265566.vov)) and **390,296 SSW holders** ([Nippon.com](https://www.nippon.com/en/news/yjj2026032700648)) will face a new Japanese-language requirement under 育成就労 from FY2027. Already, 62% of firms provide language support ([MURC](https://www.murc.jp/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/seiken_251119_01.pdf)). Experts warn that relying on translation isolates workers and recommend yasashii nihongo instead ([Tokio-dR](https://www.tokio-dr.jp/publication/column/149.html)). That turns Beekeeper's 200-language translation from a competitor into the counter-example. The agent rewrites a manager's instruction into easy Japanese at the right JLPT level, with furigana and a native-language gloss. It builds spaced-repetition micro-lessons from that week's real workplace phrases and turns the worker's replies into polite Japanese. Evidence is strong and the deadline is dated. Two things weigh against it: the expected crowding of the Future of Work track, and its heavy overlap with Manufacturing's Genba Proof.

### Idea 2 — "Nemawashi Navigator": pre-ringi stakeholder simulation

Ringi drafting is crowded, but **simulating the approval route** is not, as far as the research could tell. ADK spawns one agent per approver, each built from that approver's past comments and rejections. The panel predicts objections, orders the nemawashi briefings, adjusts keigo to each person's rank, and keeps a record of how each concern was resolved. It is the most culturally novel idea in the track and the best multi-agent showcase. Its weaknesses are demo data that must be fabricated and an Impact score that is hard to quantify; the main evidence is pandemic-era data on hanko-driven office attendance ([Kuwait Times/AFP](https://kuwaittimes.com/traditional-japanese-seal-system-hampers-telework-for-some/)).

### Idea 3 — Code-switched commitments tracker (Hinglish, Taglish, Singlish)

On identical Hinglish audio, word error rate ranges from **27% to 70%** across models ([Shunya Labs](https://huggingface.co/shunyalabs/zero-stt-hinglish/blob/main/README.md)). It must be pitched as code-switch-faithful commitment tracking over WhatsApp voice notes. It would classify soft versus hard commitments ("dekhte hain", "sige, try ko") and keep the original quotes as evidence. Pitched as meeting notes, it reads as a clone of Fabric and Gemini in Meet. A **Japanese meeting-accessibility agent** for deaf, hard-of-hearing and neurodivergent staff is a fourth option. It would decode indirect keigo (検討します ≈ "probably no") and flag questions addressed to the user, which ties to the **2.7% disability quota from July 2026** ([Adnkronos/Jiji](https://english.adnkronos.com/2026/07/06/japan-firms-face-need-to-improve-jobs-for-disabled-workers/)). No primary user evidence was gathered for it.

### Idea 1 — Citizen photo and voice to auto-filled RDANA reports for Philippine LGUs (track pick)

Typhoon Kalmaegi (November 2025) left **250 dead, 5.18M affected and 110.3K houses damaged**, and Fung-wong followed within days ([AHA Centre](https://ahacentre.org/weekly-disaster-update/weekly-disaster-update-3-9-november-2025/)). RA 10121 requires LDRRMCs to institutionalise RDANA, and LGUs still train for it manually in 2025 ([LRTA](https://www.lrta.gov.ph/rapid-damage-assessment-and-needs-analysis-rdana-training-august-19-22-2025/)). SKAI scores buildings from overhead imagery but does not capture ground-level needs (water, shelter, medical) or produce the official form. Tagalog or Cebuano voice notes and geotagged photos would flow through Gemini, be cross-checked against Open Buildings and Sentinel change detection in Earth Engine, and land in BigQuery. The output is a pre-filled RDANA PDF and a barangay needs dashboard. It has the deepest stack in any track and real Kalmaegi situation reports as demo data. Its weakness is the crowded track, and no exhaustive product search was done.

### Idea 2 — Preventing "disaster-related deaths" among elderly and foreign evacuees (Japan)

At Noto, **disaster-related deaths (397) exceeded direct deaths (228)**, and more than 90% of them were people aged 70+ ([nippon.com](https://www.nippon.com/en/in-depth/d01152/failures-of-noto-disaster-relief-drive-major-reforms-japan%E2%80%99s-revised-laws-target-infrast.html)). More than 60% of foreign residents cited language barriers and lack of information as reasons they avoided shelters ([PMC](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC12095853/)). Today's tools cover alerts, not ongoing welfare. A Gemini Live check-in agent working in Japanese dialects, Vietnamese, Indonesian and Nepali would triage daily health and needs (medication, DVT risk, dehydration, isolation) into a welfare-staff risk queue, with Gemma running on-device when the network is down. It has the most emotionally compelling evidence in the research.

### Idea 3 — Heat-stress work-rest coach for informal outdoor workers

ILO finds **74.7% of Asia-Pacific workers exposed to excessive heat**, and 8 in 10 occupational heat injuries happen *outside* declared heatwaves ([ILO](https://www.ilo.org/resource/news/more-workers-ever-are-losing-fight-against-heat-stress)). PAGASA's iHeatMap is a desktop map, not a coach for an individual worker. The build combines a voice agent with Earth Engine land-surface temperature and OSM cooling points to produce personal work-rest-hydrate schedules. It also fits Future of Work, but that track is equally crowded. A **disability-inclusive early-warning translator** (CAP alerts turned into easy-read text, pictograms and per-household action plans) is a fourth option. People with disabilities die at 2-4 times the rate of others in many disasters ([UN DESA](https://www.un.org/en/development/desa/news/social/inclusion-saves-lives.html)).

## Day-one checks decide whether any of this ships

The calendar is tighter than it looks. Today is Oct 7. **Team changes close Oct 11**, and the submission deadline is Oct 18. Judging runs Oct 19 to Nov 6 against the deployed link. **Gemini 2.5 Pro, Flash and Flash-Lite retire on Vertex on Oct 16, 2026** ([Vertex release notes](https://docs.cloud.google.com/vertex-ai/generative-ai/docs/release-notes?hl=en)), so a 2.5-based build would break while judges are testing it. The Gemini API models page lists IDs such as `gemini-3.8-flash`, `gemini-3.8-live` and `gemini-3.8-flash-tts` ([Gemini API models](https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs/models)), but their availability on Vertex and in your region was **not confirmed**. On day one, list the models the project can actually call in its region, keep the model ID in an environment variable, and smoke-test the deployed Cloud Run service rather than only a local build. The $300 trial credit does not cover Gemini API calls made through AI Studio ([Google Cloud Free](https://docs.cloud.google.com/free)), so choose Vertex billing or the free tier deliberately. The rules also say "everything must be in English". Multilingual output is the point of most of these ideas, but the UI chrome, deck and narration must be in English, with subtitles over Japanese, Tagalog or Hindi audio. A sensible split for #1 is: days 1-2 for model and language smoke tests plus evidence extraction; days 3-5 for the jurisdiction playbooks and RAG; days 6-7 for the dispute-letter generator and countdown UX; day 8 for the mule-cluster console; and days 9-11 for deployment hardening, the demo video and the deck. Cut the elder voice mode first if the schedule slips.

## Conclusion

The research changes the question from "what can Gemini do that nobody sells?" to "**at what moment do the incumbents stop?**" Fraud vendors stop at the transfer. Translation vendors stop at the translated word. SOP vendors stop at the authored manual. Google's disaster platforms stop at the map. Each winning idea above picks up at that handoff: recovering the money, proving the worker understood, filling the official form, coaching the human host who must still speak live. That framing also protects a team against late discoveries like Teachme AI, because an idea anchored to an outcome (money recovered, comprehension verified, report filed) does not lose its point when someone else ships a step that comes before it.

A second, less obvious conclusion is that **regulation now creates JAPAC whitespace faster than startups fill it**. AFASA, the SPF Act, Vietnam's Law 122, Indonesia's halal transition, 育成就労 and CBAM all took effect or set deadlines in a 15-month window, and each created a new obligation that SMEs, small banks or individuals cannot meet without help. The judges' Impact criterion rewards exactly that kind of "why now". The team that names a specific clause, a specific user and a specific deadline in the first 20 seconds of its demo video will stand out in a field of generic copilots.
