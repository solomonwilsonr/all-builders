# Capture the retiring expert before the knowledge walks out

The best open problem for a 2-4 person team to build in 10 days sits where three needs meet: **no sensors or MES (only a phone camera and voice), a multilingual or low-literacy migrant workforce, and pricing an SME can pay in JAPAC**. The strongest single idea is a **phone-video "skill capture" agent**. A veteran films and narrates a setup or repair in Japanese or Korean. Gemini turns the footage into a timestamped, visual micro-SOP in Vietnamese, Indonesian, Nepali and other worker languages, then checks a novice's attempt video against the expert's and quizzes the worker by voice. The evidence for the gap is strong. About **80% of Japanese manufacturers worry about skills transfer** ([MONOist](https://monoist.itmedia.co.jp/mn/spv/2410/28/news002.html)), **1.27M ageing SME owners have no successor** ([WEF](https://weforum.org/stories/2025/04/japan-business-succession-traditional)), and Korean migrant workers suffer about **10% of industrial fatalities while making up under 5% of the workforce** ([Korea Times](https://www.koreatimes.co.kr/southkorea/20260908/incheon-uses-game-show-format-in-safety-training-for-migrant-factory-workers)). Today the only people comparing expert and novice video are large firms running their own pilots (MHI/Algomatic, Hitachi, DENSO), and nobody sells it as an SME product. Behind the top pick, the ranked shortlist is: paper check-sheet digitisation, sensorless diagnosis of legacy machines by phone, a CBAM evidence pack built from SME paperwork, and supplier-quality (PPAP/8D) document drafting. The "chat with your plant data" copilot, sensor-based predictive maintenance, drawing-based quoting and voice shift handover are all crowded, and teams should avoid them. Two logistics points matter more than the idea. **Team registration closes Oct 11**, and **Gemini 2.5 retires on Oct 16**, two days before submission and in the middle of judging. Any build has to target a verified Gemini 3.x model ID.

## The SME problem is missing data, not weak models

Across JAPAC the same three pains keep coming up. People and their skills are leaving. Export compliance now demands data the SME never collected. And the digital systems they did install sit unused. Korea shows the third pain clearly. Only **0.1% of Korean SMEs use AI on the production floor**, **99.8% of smart factories run only partially**, and the average SME smart-factory project costs **KRW 750M, 56.9% of it self-funded** ([Asia Economy](https://view.asiae.co.kr/en/article/2025042809081811865)). Korean researchers put the low utilisation down mainly to systems that ignored what users actually needed ([KoreaScience](https://koreascience.or.kr/article/JAKO202316665173135.pdf)). In Japan, about 90% of SMEs say DX is necessary but only about 20% report results ([DX Magazine](https://dxmagazine.jp/news/2529ko28/)). The top barrier is lack of capable staff, cited by **54.1%** ([Newswitch/TDB](https://newswitch.jp/p/35534)). In India, **84% of "evolving" MSMEs see limited value in digital tools** ([CMR](https://cmrindia.com/67-of-msmes-demonstrate-digital-readiness-across-core-and-advanced-technologies-finds-cmr-study/)). Globally, **54% of small and mid-size plants still run on pen, paper or spreadsheets as their MES** ([IoT Analytics](https://iot-analytics.com/mes-vendors-replace-pen-paper-spreadsheets/)).

That changes what a winning entry looks like. The incumbents ship products that assume the factory is already digitised. Microsoft's Factory Operations Agent queries combined OT/IT, MES and QMS data ([AI Business](https://aibusiness.com/generative-ai/microsoft-launches-new-ai-agents-for-factory-automation-hannover-messe-2025)). Cognite Atlas AI sits on top of Cognite Data Fusion ([FitGap](https://us.fitgap.com/products/031793/cognite-atlas-ai)). Tractian targets firms with **100+ employees and 10+ maintenance staff** ([SoftwareConnect](https://softwareconnect.com/go/tractian-review-dddae)). MaintainX's AI CoPilot appears to be available only on Enterprise plans ([Fabrico](https://www.fabrico.io/blog/maintainx-pricing-guide-2026-free-tier-limites-paid-plans-and-real-per-user-costs/)). An SME has none of those inputs. What it does have is what a multimodal model reads natively: phone video, voice in Japanese or Hindi, scanned manuals, handwritten forms, and electricity bills. The pitch that fits the gap is "no new hardware, no data migration, works in the worker's language, delivers value in week one." The reviews that were found say adoption fails on the frontline and on sensor prerequisites, not on model quality ([SoftwareFinder on Augury](https://softwarefinder.com/facility-management-software/augury/reviews); [MAPCON](https://www.mapcon.com/blog/2025/12/ai-challenges-and-practical-fixes-for-modern-cmms-platforms)). That backs low-friction input over smarter analytics.

## Six manufacturing gaps survive the competitor screen

The ranking weighs four things: how strong the JAPAC evidence is, how thin the competition is, whether a team can ship it on Cloud Run in 10 days, and how much of the 40% Technical/GenAI and 25% Innovation weight it can win. Gemini's long-video, audio and document understanding is the common lever. A 1M-context model handles up to **an hour of high-resolution video at about 300 tokens per second** ([Gemini video docs](https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs/video-understanding)). ADK 2.x deploys multi-agent apps to Cloud Run or Agent Engine ([Google Developers Blog](https://developers.googleblog.com/agents-adk-agent-engine-a2a-enhancements-google-io/)).

### 1. Skill-capture agent: expert video to multilingual micro-SOP with a novice check (top pick)

**Gap evidence.** Japan's skills crisis is structural. About **2.45M SME owners are projected to be over 70**, about **1.27M of them with no successor**, and that puts about **6.5M jobs and ¥22T of GDP** at risk ([WEF](https://weforum.org/stories/2025/04/japan-business-succession-traditional)). The 2024 Monozukuri White Paper defines skills as tacit, experience-based knowledge and finds **about 80% of manufacturers worried about passing them on** ([MONOist](https://monoist.itmedia.co.jp/mn/spv/2410/28/news002.html)). The people receiving those skills increasingly do not speak Japanese. Japan has about **2.6M foreign workers, with manufacturing the top sector** ([Malay Mail](https://www.malaymail.com/news/money/2025/01/31/japans-foreign-workforce-hits-all-time-high-as-labour-gaps-widen/165069)), and only about **60% of one Vietnamese intern cohort reached JLPT N4** ([Vietnam News](https://vietnamnews.vn/society/1730846/viet-nam-japan-eye-stronger-labour-cooperation.html)).

**Why competitors fall short.** The closest work is MHI and Algomatic's GENIAC-PRIZE entry, which compares expert and novice TIG-welding video. It is an enterprise R&D result, not a product ([MHI](https://www.mhi.com/news/26040801.html)). Hitachi builds GenAI virtual training sites for its own retirement wave ([KrASIA](https://kr-asia.com/hitachi-to-use-generative-ai-to-pass-expert-skills-to-next-generation)). Video-to-SOP tools such as Guidde cover *software* screen recordings. SOPs generated from shop-floor video of physical tasks still sit mainly in research ([Guidde](https://www.guidde.com/knowledge-hub/ai-powered-sop-generation-platforms-guide); [arXiv 2409.15867](https://arxiv.org/html/2409.15867v5)). Tulip starts from PDFs or descriptions, not from video ([Tulip](https://tulip.co/lp/ai-work-instructions/)). The research found **no SME-priced Japanese tacit-knowledge product**, though that absence is not proven.

**10-day build.** Run a pipeline of ADK agents on Cloud Run. Agent one ingests a 3-5 minute phone video with Japanese narration and extracts steps, timestamps, keyframes, tolerances, and "why" notes, such as "listen for this sound before tightening." Agent two writes a simplified visual SOP in a target language and reads it aloud with Gemini TTS. Agent three takes a novice's attempt video and returns a step-by-step deviation report that points to timestamps in both videos. An optional Live API voice quiz then checks comprehension in the worker's language. Firestore holds the SOP library. **Data:** the team films its own clips (a bicycle chain change, a drill-press setup, a filter swap), which avoids licensing issues. **Risk:** step alignment between two videos has to be robust. Use short, controlled clips and let Gemini do the comparison through structured JSON output rather than building custom vision models.

### 2. Migrant-worker safety comprehension checker

**Gap evidence.** In Korea, accidents among foreign workers "stem from an inability to read safety signs, operate emergency stop mechanisms or fully grasp verbal instructions" ([Korea Times](https://www.koreatimes.co.kr/southkorea/20260908/incheon-uses-game-show-format-in-safety-training-for-migrant-factory-workers)). **18.7% of surveyed migrant shipyard workers were trained in a language they did not understand** ([BHRRC](https://www.business-humanrights.org/ar/أحدث-الأخبار/s-korea-report-highlights-safety-blind-spots-for-subcontracted-and-migrant-workers-in-shipyards)). Large firms make safety videos in 11 languages, which SMEs cannot afford ([Korea Times](https://www.koreatimes.co.kr/business/companies/20240712/hd-hyundai-daewoo-ec-lower-language-barriers-at-workplaces)). Malaysia and Taiwan report the same failure mode ([Emerald](https://emerald.com/insight/content/doi/10.1108/S2040-726220220000026019); [PMC](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC4962247/)).

**Why competitors fall short.** Microsoft's Factory Safe Agent targets OHS guidelines and inspections at enterprises that already run Microsoft stacks ([AI Business](https://aibusiness.com/generative-ai/microsoft-launches-new-ai-agents-for-factory-automation-hannover-messe-2025)). Augmentir lists multi-language support, but English is the primary listed language in regional listings ([Capterra SG](https://www.capterra.com.sg/software/191937/augmentir)). Translation alone is not the gap. The gap is **verifying that a Nepali or Burmese worker understood the lockout procedure**, and keeping an auditable record that they did.

**10-day build.** A supervisor photographs a Korean or Japanese SOP or warning sign. Gemini produces a pictogram-led, plain-language version in the worker's language, then runs a spoken quiz through the Live API and logs a pass/fail record per worker. **Data:** OSHA Severe Injury Reports narratives can seed realistic hazard scenarios and quiz questions ([OSHA SIR](https://osha.gov/severe-injury-reports)). This idea and idea #1 share most of their components. **They work best merged into one product:** capture the procedure from the expert, deliver it in the worker's language, and verify that the worker understood it.

### 3. Paper check-sheet digitiser with drift alerts

**Gap evidence.** With **54% of SME plants running on paper or spreadsheets** ([IoT Analytics](https://iot-analytics.com/mes-vendors-replace-pen-paper-spreadsheets/)) and MES projects failing on cost and fit in Korea ([Asia Economy](https://view.asiae.co.kr/en/article/2025042809081811865)), the cheapest route to data is the clipboard the shop already uses. A phone photo of a handwritten inspection sheet or shift log becomes structured rows in BigQuery. Gemini flags out-of-spec values and trends, and drafts a nonconformance note.

**Why competitors fall short.** MES vendors sell replacement of paper, not digitisation of it as it stands ([IoT Analytics](https://iot-analytics.com/mes-vendors-replace-pen-paper-spreadsheets/)). The research did not identify a product focused on handwritten Japanese/Thai/Vietnamese check-sheets for SMEs. That means **competitive coverage is unverified**, and generic OCR/IDP vendors may partly cover it. Novelty is moderate. Buildability and impact are high.

**Data:** render synthetic handwritten sheets from AI4I 2020 values (CC BY 4.0) so that the demo's drift alerts match a known ground truth ([UCI AI4I](https://archive.ics.uci.edu/dataset/601)).

### 4. Sensorless legacy-machine diagnosis from phone audio and video

**Gap evidence.** For small workshops, sensors and data infrastructure for predictive maintenance "may never pay back" ([Alibaba SEA blog, vendor source](https://seller.alibaba.com/blogs/2026/southeast-asia/apparel-accessories/predictive-maintenance-optimization-guide-alibaba-b2b)). Practitioners estimate that replacing a 30-year technician takes 18 months instead of 6, because diagnostic shortcuts were never written down ([UpKeep](https://upkeep.com/blog/maintenance-knowledge-loss-why-hiring-does-not-replace-a-retiring-technician/), vendor blog).

**Why competitors fall short.** Tractian, Augury and Senseye all depend on sensors or condition-monitoring data ([Tractian](https://tractian.com/en/smart-trac/sensor-pricing); [Siemens](https://news.siemens.com/da-dk/siemens-elevates-predictive-maintenance-with-innovative-generative-ai/)). MachineMetrics needs an edge device ([Automate.org](https://www.automate.org/ai/news/machinemetrics-unveils-revolutionary-ai-driven-tool-monitoring-to-predict-and-prevent-failures-machinemetrics)). No commercial phone-microphone diagnostic for SMEs was found, though that absence was not confirmed by a targeted search.

**10-day build.** A technician records a 10-second clip of the machine, photographs the HMI or analog gauge, and states the symptom. Gemini combines that with RAG over the scanned legacy manual and past repair notes and returns ranked likely causes and next checks. **Data:** MIMII or the smaller DCASE 2020 Task 2 dev set for pump, fan and valve sounds (CC BY-SA) ([Zenodo MIMII](https://zenodo.org/record/3384387); [DCASE](https://zenodo.org/record/3678170)), and MaintNet's 6,169 aviation records for free-text repair history ([Baselight](https://baselight.app/u/kaggle/dataset/merishnasuwal_aircraft_historical_maintenance_dataset)). **Risk:** Gemini's raw audio-anomaly accuracy is unproven. Position the tool as triage, and consider a small classical anomaly score that Gemini then explains. This idea also edges toward the crowded maintenance category.

### 5. CBAM evidence pack from SME paperwork

**Gap evidence.** The CBAM payment phase started on 1 Jan 2026. **3,000-4,000 Indian steel and aluminium firms export to the EU directly, and 25,000-30,000 more export indirectly** ([Mongabay India](https://india.mongabay.com/2026/06/european-union-border-tax-tests-indias-small-iron-and-steel-businesses/)). Compliance costs about **₹15-20 lakh per MSME**. Without actual data, the EU applies default values with mark-ups of **10% in 2026, 20% in 2027 and 30% from 2028**. Industry's own diagnosis is that "the biggest challenge is data, not technology" ([Business Today](https://www.businesstoday.in/amp/industry/story/eus-new-carbon-tax-could-hit-thousands-of-indian-msmes-can-they-adapt-536301-2026-06-11)). In practice, MSMEs fill in importer forms by hand from electricity bills and production logs ([Mongabay India](https://india.mongabay.com/2026/06/european-union-border-tax-tests-indias-small-iron-and-steel-businesses/)).

**Why competitors fall short, and why it ranks only fifth.** The calculators are already served. The Sustainability Cloud has plans from €0 and a Vietnam engine, Korea's CBAM-PASS is free, and CbamTrack has a free SME tier ([ALCircle](https://www.alcircle.com/news/new-cbam-software-offers-cost-effective-carbon-reporting-for-smes-and-enterprises-113394); [cbamguide](https://cbamguide.com/news/2026-09-02-korea-13th-joint-cbam-briefing-15-support-measures/)). The remaining gap is narrower: **reading messy, multilingual bills, invoices and production registers into the activity data those calculators need**, with an audit trail back to each source document. **Data:** the EU definitive-period default values in Excel ([tulli.fi](https://tulli.fi/en/-/the-default-values-and-benchmarks-for-the-cbam-definitive-period-have-been-published-in-excel-format-there-are-also-new-guidance-videos-on-the-cbam-registry)) and the `opencbam` package ([PyPI](https://pypi.org/project/opencbam/)). This idea fits the Sustainability theme equally well, which helps with positioning. But judges may see it as a document-extraction exercise.

### 6. PPAP / 8D drafting assistant for Tier-2/3 auto suppliers

**Gap evidence.** PPAP has 18 elements, Level 3 is the default expectation for Tier 2/3 suppliers, and late PPAP costs suppliers program awards ([KlearStack](https://klearstack.com/blogs/iatf-16949-software); [Alibaba SEA guide](https://seller.alibaba.com/blogs/2026/southeast-asia/automotive/iatf-16949-ppap-supplier-guide-alibaba)). Both sources are vendor marketing, and **no independent JAPAC survey was found**. Supplier-quality and 8D-writing startups were not researched, so competitive density is unknown. A Gemini agent that drafts PFMEA rows, control plans and 8D reports from a drawing, process notes and defect photos is easy to build. It ranks last because the evidence and the competitor scan are both the weakest of the six.

## Media whitespace favours professional workflows over generation

In the Media, Content & Digital Experiences theme the saturated zone is generation itself. HeyGen claims 175+ dubbing languages, Rask AI 130+, and CAMB.AI 150+ ([CAMB.AI](https://www.camb.ai/blog-post/rask-ai-alternatives)). News summarisation by chatbots is already mainstream, with 18% weekly use in India ([Reuters Institute DNR 2025](https://reutersinstitute.politics.ox.ac.uk/digital-news-report/2025/dnr-executive-summary)). Google's own ABC Australia case tagged about 1M archive records with Gemini ([Google Cloud](https://cloud.google.com/customers/abc?hl=fr)). **Avoid:** dubbing, voice cloning, avatars, clip and shorts generators, generic summarisers, large-broadcaster archive tagging, and Japanese Sign Language avatars (KIKI already exists) ([Cannes Lions](https://lovethework.com/work-awards/campaigns/kiki-virtual-human-interpreter-1628115)).

The open space is human-in-the-loop tools for specific APAC professionals. The best is **vernacular video claim triage for fact-checkers**. Social video news use rose from 52% to 65% between 2020 and 2025, and TikTok reaches 49% of Thais for news ([DNR 2025](https://reutersinstitute.politics.ox.ac.uk/digital-news-report/2025/dnr-executive-summary)). Philippine fact-checkers say most AI tools are "programmed exclusively in English" and reject full automation ([VERA Files](https://verafiles.org/articles/ph-fact-checkers-wary-of-ai-for-fact-checking)), though that evidence dates from 2023. Gemini can ingest a Taglish or Hinglish video, extract timestamped claims, match them against ClaimReview data, and draft a verdict for a human checker. Second is **audio description for Asian-language film and TV**. Blind audiences in South Asia have "almost no access" to it, and Nobaflix's 500+ titles is the main counter-example ([Zero Project](https://zeroproject.org/view/project/23c0d84c-1e41-f011-8779-7c1e527683f1)). Gemini can find gaps in the dialogue and write culturally aware descriptions to fill them, which Cloud TTS then voices. Third is **Yasashii Nihongo (plain Japanese) conversion** of news and disaster alerts for foreign residents. IEICE published research on this in 2026, which shows the problem is still unsolved ([IEICE](https://ken.ieice.org/ken/paper/20260704BcvY/eng/)). This idea connects directly to the manufacturing migrant-worker story. Two more ideas carry weaker evidence. One is cultural and regulatory localisation QA for ads aimed at ID, MY, TH, VN and JP. This rests on inference, with no third-party demand data. The other is mother-tongue learning materials for the Philippines. Demand there may have dropped after a 2024 law change, which is unverified. If a team prefers the Media theme, claim triage is the strongest entry, since it relies on Gemini's native video and audio understanding in a way text-only tools cannot copy.

## Conclusion

The research points to a counter-intuitive conclusion. For JAPAC SMEs, the winning AI product is not a smarter analytics layer. It is a capture layer that turns phone video, voice and paper into structured knowledge in the worker's own language. **Build the merged skill-capture and comprehension agent**: a veteran films a procedure, Gemini produces a visual micro-SOP in Vietnamese, Indonesian or Nepali, a novice's attempt is checked against the expert video, and a voice quiz confirms the worker understood. It answers two documented crises at once, Japan's succession cliff and migrant-worker safety. The only comparable work is enterprise R&D at MHI and Hitachi. And it uses Gemini's long-video, audio, TTS and Live capabilities in ways a judge will recognise as more than superficial GenAI. A sensible 10-day sequence is: lock the team and register by **Oct 11**; days 1-3 build the video-to-SOP agent; days 4-6 build the novice comparison and translation; days 7-8 add the Live quiz and the Cloud Run/Firebase deploy; days 9-10 cover the demo video, deck and repo. Cut the voice quiz first if the schedule slips.

**Check model IDs before writing any code.** Gemini 2.5 Pro, Flash and Flash-Lite **retire on Vertex on Oct 16, 2026** ([Vertex release notes](https://docs.cloud.google.com/vertex-ai/generative-ai/docs/release-notes?hl=en)). That is two days before submission and before judging runs Oct 19-Nov 6, so a 2.5-based deployment would break while judges are testing it. The Gemini API models page lists stable IDs such as `gemini-3.8-flash`, `gemini-3.8-live` and `gemini-3.8-flash-tts` ([Gemini API models](https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs/models)). However, the Vertex release notes fetched for this research did not confirm the 3.6-3.8 entries, so availability on Vertex and in your region is **unverified**. On day one, list the models your project can actually call in its region. Keep the model ID in an environment variable. Run a smoke test against the deployed Cloud Run service, not only locally. One more cost detail: the $300 Google Cloud trial credit cannot pay for Gemini API calls made through AI Studio ([Google Cloud Free](https://docs.cloud.google.com/free)). Choose either Vertex billed against the credit or the Gemini API free tier, and keep demo videos short at low media resolution.
