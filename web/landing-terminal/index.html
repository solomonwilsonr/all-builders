How it
works

  1. Page The extension reads the product area and filters out names, addresses and phone numbers.
  2. Engine Cloud Run matches the listing in a catalog of 27,000+ listings, with Gemini on Vertex AI.
  3. Records Recalls, hazard reports, safety marks, photos, price and shop age are checked.
  4. Verdict Code sets the colour. Gemini writes the explanation and must cite evidence that exists.

Frequently asked

Does it see my personal details?

No. Kasauti reads only the product area of the page and filters out names, addresses, pincodes, emails and phone numbers before anything is sent. Nothing is sent until you press Check.

Does it work on my phone?

Not as an extension: Chrome on Android, iPhone and iPad cannot add browser extensions, so Kasauti runs in Chrome on a computer. For phones, a WhatsApp version is built and waiting for its public number: send a product link or a photo, and get the answer back as text and a voice note.

What if it is not sure?

It says "Not enough evidence yet" in grey, never green. A product it cannot match exactly gets no safety claim, because a look-alike's recall is not this product's recall.

Can my agent use it?

Yes. The same answers are served as MCP tools at /mcp and as REST endpoints such as POST /v1/listings/trust-check. All the MCP tools are read-only.

Is the verdict a legal finding?

No. It is evidence for a shopper: official recalls, review reports under review, and checks on photos, price and seller, each with its source.

How accurate is it?

On 25 real recalls and 40 never-recalled products, it raised 2 false alarms against 11 for keyword rules. On the recalls it was not tuned on, it caught 2 of 9 with no false alarms. We publish both numbers.

Why the name?

A kasauti is the black touchstone a jeweller rubs gold against. Real gold leaves a streak of its true colour; brass does not.
