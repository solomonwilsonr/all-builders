FAQs
Questions about privacy, phones, accuracy or plugging in your own agent.

      

        
What is SafeShelf?
A product check for online shoppers. It answers two questions about any listing: is it safe (recalls, hazard reports, safety marks) and is it real (copied photos, impossible price, brand-new shop), with the evidence for every line.

        
Does it see my personal details?
No. It reads only the product area of the page and filters out names, addresses, pincodes, emails and phone numbers before anything is sent. Nothing is sent until you press Check.

        
Does it work on my phone?
Not as an extension: Chrome on phones cannot add extensions. A WhatsApp version is built and waiting for its public number: send a product link or a photo, and get text and a voice note back.

        
What if it is not sure?
It says "Not enough evidence yet" in grey, never green. A product it cannot match exactly gets no safety claim, because a look-alike's recall is not this product's recall.

        
How accurate is it?
On 25 real recalls and 40 never-recalled products, it raised 2 false alarms against 6 for Gemini alone and 11 for keyword rules. Keyword rules catch more recalls. On the recalls it was not tuned on, it caught 2 of 9 with no false alarms. All the numbers.

        
Is the verdict a legal finding?
No. It is evidence for a shopper: official recalls, reports under review, and checks on photos, price and seller, each with its source.
