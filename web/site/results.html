Do Buyer Reviews
Warn Before a Recall?

25 real US recalls (CPSC) and 40 products that were never recalled. Each method reads only the reviews written before the recall date. We count the recalls it would have flagged, and the safe products it would have wrongly flagged.

Keyword rules11/40
Gemini alone6/40
SafeShelf2/40

False alarms on 40 never-recalled products. Shorter is better.

MethodRecalls flaggedMedian warningFalse alarms
Keyword rules11/25170 weeks11/40
Gemini alone6/25104 weeks6/40
SafeShelf6/2593 weeks2/40
SafeShelf, without pooling copies1/2567 weeks2/40

"Median warning" is how long before the official recall the first warning would have appeared. Pooling the reviews of every copy of the same product is what moves SafeShelf from 1 recall to 6.

Where we fall short. Keyword rules flag more recalls (11 against our 6) and earlier, at the cost of 11 false alarms. On the half of the recalls we did not tune on, SafeShelf flagged 2 of 9 with no false alarms on 14 safe products. The sample is small, and we publish both numbers.

Early work

We found 19 products recalled in one country and later in another, mostly the US first and Australia later. The second recall came a median of 22 days after the first, and up to 293 days.
