        Step 7 of 7

        Why trust it

        Gemini does the reading, searching and talking. Plain code makes the final call, so it cannot invent a recall.

        What Gemini does

        • Reads 29,379 buyer reviewsand review photos, for fire, burn and shock reports
        • Reads the labelfrom a photo: brand, model, ratings, safety marks
        • Finds the genuine productwith Google Search, for products never seen before
        • Holds the conversationGemini Live by voice; chat with sources in 26 languages
        • Spots tricks aimed at AIhidden instructions in listings, fake regulator pages

        What code decides

        • Sets the colourred, amber, green or grey, from the evidence alone
        • Decides what countsonly an exact model inherits a recall
        • Says “can't tell yet”when the evidence is thin, instead of guessing

        Two questions about every listing, then one of four answers, each with the evidence behind it.

        It asks

        Is it safe?

        • Official recalls from the US and 7 Asia-Pacific regulators, India included
        • Buyer reviews and photos, read for fire, burn and shock
        • Safety marks, such as BIS, against the registry
        and

        Is it real?

        • Photos copied from the official listing
        • A price far below the genuine product
        • A brand-new shop, and hidden text aimed at AI tools
        You get one answer
        • Don't buyrecalled or fake
        • Be carefulreports under review
        • No problems foundin what was checked
        • Can't tell yetnever a guess

        Code picks the answer. Plain rules turn the evidence into a colour; Gemini only explains it, in your language.

        Exact model only. A look-alike's recall is never blamed on the wrong product.

        Tested on 25 real recalls and 40 products that were never recalled, against Gemini alone and keyword rules.
