      Why trust it

      How it decides, how it scored against Gemini alone, and what happened when we attacked it.

      Two questions about every listing, then one of four answers, each with the evidence behind it.

      It asks

      Is it safe?

      • Official recalls from the US and 7 Asia-Pacific regulators, India included
      • Buyer reviews and photos, read for fire, burn and shock
      • Safety marks, such as BIS, against the registry
      and

      Is it real?

      • Photos copied from the official listing
      • A price far below the genuine product
      • A brand-new shop, and hidden text aimed at AI tools
      You get one answer
      • Don't buyrecalled or fake
      • Be carefulreports under review
      • No problems foundin what was checked
      • Can't tell yetnever a guess

      Code picks the answer. Plain rules turn the evidence into a colour; Gemini only explains it, in your language.

      Exact model only. A look-alike's recall is never blamed on the wrong product.
