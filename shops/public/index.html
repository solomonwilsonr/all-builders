Seven shop pages where the right answer is known

Real web pages for the seven test products on the judges' page: a recall in Japan, a US recall sold in India, a fake shop, a borrowed safety mark, melting chargers, a product nobody has data on, and a clean control that must stay quiet.

How to use these shops

  1. 1

    In the judges' page real browser

    On the judges' page, press Start a real browser. A real Chrome with SafeShelf opens on this page. Pick a product below, or tap Real browser on any product card there.

  2. 2

    In your own Chrome

    Add the SafeShelf extension and sign in, then open any product below.

  3. 3

    Press the shield, read the answer

    On a product page, press the shield button, Check with SafeShelf, on the right edge. Each page has a short guide saying what a correct answer is, so you can tell at once if SafeShelf got it right.

These checks run live on SafeShelf's API and use one check each. For instant answers with no extension, use the test pages on the judges' page: the same seven listings.
