# Prior-Art Check: LiveGuard (Retail, SEA)

**Date of check:** 2026-10-08
**Idea:** A real-time compliance and Q&A "whisperer" for human live-commerce hosts on TikTok Shop, Shopee Live and Lazada Live in Vietnam, Indonesia, Thailand and the Philippines. It listens to the host's speech and the live chat, flags misleading, false or unregistered product claims as they are spoken (under Vietnam E-commerce Law 122/2025, TikTok Shop policy, and cosmetics/supplement claim rules), and puts compliant answers to viewer questions on the host's screen.

## Verdict: PARTIALLY DONE

Every core piece already exists somewhere. Real-time ASR violation alerts to the host exist (mainly in China). LLM-based claim judgement exists. Suggested replies to chat questions exist. At least one Chinese platform (数链智联, June 2026) and one open-source Douyin project (智农直播助手, June 2026) combine compliance checking with live assistance. **No one we found has built this for SEA languages, SEA platforms or Vietnam's Law 122/2025.** Nor have we found a product that pairs claim-checking against registered product data (e.g., cosmetics notification or supplement registration) with Q&A suggestions in one host-facing overlay. The pitch can claim novelty on localization, regulation and product-registration grounding. It cannot claim the core concept as new.

**Name collision:** NexTide Media already launched a product called **"Liveguard"** (Oct 2025), a real-time AI brand-safety monitor for livestreams. Consider renaming, e.g., "LiveGuard SEA", "Lời Chuẩn", "HostShield" or "ClaimCheck Live".

## 3. Context that strengthens the pitch

- **TikTok Shop now bans AI-generated voices and pre-recorded audio in shopping lives** (enforced via Creator Health Rating), and "AI can help prepare the stream but can't replace the real-time interaction inside it." AI-avatar hosts such as AnyLive are therefore constrained, and the value moves to tools that assist a *human* host. LiveGuard fits this exactly: the AI whispers to the host and never speaks on stream. (https://www.switcherstudio.com/whats-new/tiktok-bans-ai-voices-from-shopping-livestreams, https://ppc.land/tiktok-shops-quality-rules-ban-ai-voices-and-still-images-from-lives/)
- **Vietnam Law 122/2025/QH15** took effect 2026-07-01. It makes livestream presenters and KOLs/KOCs liable for false or misleading information on uses, origin, quality, price, promotions and warranty, with penalties equivalent to sellers. Sellers must also supply product-quality and advertising-content documents before going live. (https://english.luatvietnam.vn/law-on-e-commerce-no-122-2025-qh15-dated-december-10-2025-of-the-national-assembly-423356-doc1.html, https://vietnamnews.vn/economy/1725726/viet-nam-s-upcoming-e-commerce-law-to-tighten-rules-on-livestream-sellers.html)
- Industry commentary (Campaign Asia) says platforms catch the "scary stuff" but not "someone over-claiming a face cream's benefits", and that Douyin, Taobao, Shopee and TikTok lack real-time claim compliance.

## 4. What is genuinely novel (differentiation for the pitch)

1. **SEA multilingual and code-switched ASR plus claim reasoning** (Vietnamese, Bahasa, Thai, Taglish), including slang and fast "chốt đơn" sales-patter speech. All real-time host-speech compliance tools found are Chinese-language or China-platform.
2. **Grounding in SEA law and registrations**: Vietnam Law 122/2025 and Decree 147, VN cosmetics/supplement advertising rules ("thực phẩm này không phải là thuốc…" disclaimers), Indonesia BPOM and Thai FDA claim rules, and the TikTok Shop SEA prohibited-claims list. Each flag cites the specific clause.
3. **Claim-vs-registration verification**: checking the spoken claim against the product's *registered* facts (cosmetic notification number, supplement registration, declared ingredients) rather than a banned-word list. Chinese tools are mostly word lists or LLM prompts. None ground against a product registry.
4. **One host-facing "whisperer" overlay with both halves**: (a) a spoken-claim flag with a compliant rephrase the host can say immediately, and (b) compliant suggested answers to chat questions. Click-to-Ask and 智农 cover the Q&A side and some compliance, but neither checks the human host's free speech against law in real time on SEA platforms.
5. **Works on platforms the seller does not control**: TikTok Shop, Shopee Live and Lazada Live via a companion app or OBS audio tap plus chat ingestion. Polyv, Alibaba and 数链智联 run inside their own stack or Chinese platform APIs.
6. **Evidence and audit trail for Law 122 liability**: timestamped log of claims flagged and corrected, usable as a due-diligence record for brands, MCNs and KOCs.
7. **Positioning against platform enforcement**: TikTok and Shopee penalize after a violation. LiveGuard gives a heads-up before the violation-point system punishes the seller.

## 5. Recommended pitch framing

"China already has real-time livestream compliance (数链智联, Polyv, 蝉管家). SEA has none, and Vietnam's Law 122 made hosts personally liable on 1 July 2026, while TikTok banned AI voices so humans must stay on mic. LiveGuard is the first Vietnamese/Bahasa/Thai whisperer that checks what the human host actually says against the product's registered claims and the law, and hands back a compliant line and compliant answers to viewer questions within seconds."

Avoid claiming "first real-time livestream compliance AI" or "first AI live-commerce co-pilot". Both are false given the prior art above. Rename the product to avoid the NexTide "Liveguard" clash.
