# Competitor screen: candidate idea spaces (AI Builder Cup 2026)

Date: 2026-10-10. Basis: the 70 named public GitHub entries in `COMPETITOR_ENTRIES.md` (first scan, re-scan and second scan). Method: read every README and grep all of them for: health insurance, claim, pre-auth, PM-JAY, Ayushman, TPA, PhilHealth, BPJS, ASHA, barangay health, posyandu, anganwadi, community health, recall, counterfeit, NSQ, spurious, food safety, FSSAI, welfare, subsidy, scheme, migrant, OFW, work permit, hospital, clinic, patient, pharmacy, medicine. The hits were then read by hand. Web search (LinkedIn, Devpost, X) found no named team posts in these spaces.

Caveat: most teams are private until Oct 18. "Open" means only that no public entry covers the space.

## Summary
- **Open:** (b) CHW register to report, (c) medicine recall / NSQ batch check, (d) food safety recall, (e) welfare form-filling.
- **Partly taken:** (a) Kavach covers patient-side health-claim appeals in India and Singapore, but the hospital-side pre-auth, PM-JAY, PhilHealth and BPJS space is open. (f) NomadFinance covers migrant worker contracts and finances, but not permit or visa paperwork.
- **Fully taken:** none.
