# Retail & Commerce (JAPAC) — Unsolved GenAI Gaps for Google Cloud AI Builder Cup 2026

Scope: JAPAC retail/commerce gaps (2024-2026 evidence) that could be built in ~10 days by 2-4 people on Gemini/Gemma + Cloud Run/Firebase, and that are not already a shipped product. Research date: 2026-10-07. ~16 searches/fetches; evidence depth varies by gap and is flagged.

## Which GenAI retail areas are saturated (the "crowded — avoid" list)?

### Takeaway
Shopper-facing chat and discovery assistants, AI hosts for livestreams, brand-side shelf auditing, khata ledgers, WhatsApp/voice B2B ordering, and COD/RTO risk scoring all already have funded products, often from Google itself. An entry in any of these areas would be judged "derivative" on Innovation (25%).

### Cited Findings
- **Google's own retail agents (avoid shopper chat, discovery, and cart agents):** At NRF in January 2026, Google Cloud launched "Gemini Enterprise for Customer Experience". It handles product discovery, cart building, ordering and post-purchase support across channels. Kroger and Papa Johns are early deployments, and Best Buy reports a 200% rise in self-service rescheduling — [IT Brief AU](https://itbrief.com.au/story/google-launches-gemini-cx-ai-to-unite-retail-journeys); [LetsDataScience](https://letsdatascience.com/news/google-cloud-launches-gemini-enterprise-for-retail-c82ce253). Google also promotes an "Invisible Shelf"/agentic-commerce idea for CPG — [Stellagent](https://stellagent.ai/insights/google-cloud-invisible-shelf-agentic-commerce).
- **Seller-side copilots on marketplaces:** TikTok Shop's AI "Seller Assistant" in Seller Center is widely available. It answers listing questions, gives performance insights and makes optimization suggestions — [Global Dating Insights](https://www.globaldatinginsights.com/featured/tiktok-shop-rolls-out-new-tools-to-boost-seller-efficiency/).
- **AI livestream hosts/avatars:** AnyMind's AnyLive runs AI hosts in Indonesian, English, Thai and Vietnamese, integrated with TikTok Shop and Shopee, streaming 20+ hrs/day — [AnyMind](https://anymindgroup.com/news/press-release/ai_influencers_live_commerce). TikTok Shop has since **banned AI-generated voices, prerecorded audio and static images** in promotional livestreams. All speech must be real-time — [PYMNTS](https://www.pymnts.com/?p=3823468); [Switcher Studio](https://www.switcherstudio.com/whats-new/tiktok-bans-ai-voices-from-shopping-livestreams). Avatar hosting is crowded and now also restricted by the platform.
- **Brand-side shelf image recognition (planogram, share-of-shelf):** ParallelDots ShelfWatch processes more than 3M photos/month in Indian traditional trade and partners with Google Cloud — [ParallelDots](https://www.paralleldots.com/br/shelfwatch); [ParallelDots x Google Cloud](https://www.paralleldots.com/resources/blog/paralleldots-partners-with-google-cloud-to-revolutionise-retail-shelf-monitoring-using-advanced-image-recognition). Trax + Retail-Scan serve Indian FMCG — [IndiaRetailing](https://www.indiaretailing.com/?p=48632).
- **Digital khata / credit ledgers:** OkCredit has 1 crore+ shopkeepers, 11 languages, voice-note entries, UPI reminders — [OkCredit](https://okcredit.in/en/). VoiceKhata does Hindi/Hinglish voice ledgers with WhatsApp reminders — [FileHippo listing](https://filehippo.com/android/download_voicekhata-ai-bahi-khata-app/).
- **Voice-note / WhatsApp B2B ordering:** Flipkart launched voice-led wholesale ordering on WhatsApp with Sarvam AI. It covers regional languages, discovery, stock, discounts and payment — [MIT Sloan ME](https://www.mitsloanme.com/article/flipkart-brings-voice-led-wholesale-ordering-to-whatsapp-with-sarvam-ai/). Unico Connect converts WhatsApp voice notes into POs — [SpeechTech](https://www.speechtechmag.com/Articles/Editorial/Case-Studies/Unico-Connect-Builds-AI-Voice-to-Order-System-for-Ashokraj-Transport--Logistics-174957.aspx). Other players include ConvoZen's FMCG chatbot — [ConvoZen](https://convozen.ai/products/fmcg-chatbot) — and Badho.AI (Auriga) — [Auriga](https://aurigait.com/case-study/ai-whatsapp-chatbot-for-fmcg-distribution/).
- **Sari-sari / micro-retailer AI insights & forecasting:** Packworks' AI "Store Insighting Project" covers 300,000 micro-retailers. It lifted median top-SKU sales from ₱187,229 to ₱335,818 in two months and total sales by 29%. Its Sari.PH Pro app bundles pricing, inventory and working-capital loans — [Inquirer](https://plus.inquirer.net/business/ai-tool-boosts-sari-sari-store-sales/); [Manila Bulletin](https://mb.com.ph/article/10921791/business/trade-industry/ai-helps-sari-sari-stores-raise-salespackworks); [DOST-PCIEERD](https://pcieerd.dost.gov.ph/news/latest-news/575-dost-packworks-partner-for-ai-powered-sari-sari-stores).
- **COD / RTO risk:** India COD RTO is about 25-30% vs 2-3% for prepaid, and 60-65% of Indian e-com orders are COD (ET Prime 2024, as cited) — [Razorpay](https://razorpay.com/blog/cash-on-delivery-and-the-challenges/); [eChai](https://echai.ventures/d2c/d2c-rto-returns-cod/what-is-rto-why-kills-margins). Indonesia COD RTO is about 15-20% (vendor blog, weaker source) — [EasySell](https://easysellapp.com/blogs/wiki/cod-order-value-rto-rate-price-range-delivery). The problem is real, but many D2C/logistics vendors already serve it (see Gaps).
- **Counterfeit detection:** The OECD/EUIPO "Mapping Global Trade in Fakes 2025" puts fakes at about $467B in 2021. The EU 2025 watch list names Shopee and Tokopedia as weak — [Rouse](https://rouse.com/insights/news/2025/eu-s-stance-on-brand-protection-in-southeast-asia). Brand-protection firms and law firms already work this area, and a judge-able demo needs ground-truth fakes, which are hard to get.

### Inferences
- Crowded areas to avoid: generic shopper chatbots and assistants (Rufus, Sidekick, Gemini Enterprise for CX, Vertex AI Search for commerce); AI product descriptions and photography; virtual try-on; generic recommenders; demand-forecasting SaaS (RELEX, Blue Yonder); WhatsApp commerce bots; AI livestream avatars (crowded and now TikTok-banned); brand-side shelf audits; voice khata ledgers; voice-note B2B ordering; COD risk scoring; counterfeit detection.
- The pattern in what is left: **compliance-in-the-loop tools for small sellers** and **human-assist, real-time multimodal tools** (not AI replacements), especially in local languages where new 2025-2026 laws create deadlines.

### Gaps
- I did not verify Shopify Sidekick, Amazon Rufus, RELEX, Blue Yonder, virtual try-on or AI product photography vendors in this session. Their crowdedness is assumed from common knowledge.
- I found no cited list of Indian or Indonesian COD/RTO vendors in this session. The likely players (GoKwik, Shiprocket, Pickrr) are unverified here.

## Candidate gaps with evidence, existing tools, buildability and datasets (5-7)

### Takeaway
The strongest gaps come from **new regulation plus human-only rules**:
- **(1)** A real-time compliance and Q&A copilot for *human* live-commerce hosts. It is driven by Vietnam's E-commerce Law 122/2025 (effective 1 Jul 2026) and TikTok's ban on AI voices.
- **(2)** A halal and label compliance checker for Indonesian online sellers, built from packaging photos. The deadline is 17 Oct 2026, the day before the hackathon deadline.

Next come (3) a shopkeeper-side "store brain" that joins shelf photos, credit and reorders, (4) near-expiry rescue for small grocers, (5) blind-accessible voice shopping in Indian languages, and (6) multilingual clerks for understaffed Japanese shops. Evidence for (4)-(6) is thinner.

### Cited Findings

**Gap 1 — "LiveGuard": real-time compliance + chat-Q&A copilot for human live-commerce hosts (VN/ID/TH/PH)**
- Need/scale: video commerce is about 25% of SEA e-commerce GMV, and GMV grew 2.5x in two years. Sellers using video platforms rose 80% YoY to more than 3M stores, and SEA e-com GMV is about $185B in 2025 — [Bain e-Conomy SEA 2025](https://www.bain.com/about/media-center/press-releases/sea/e-conomy-sea-2025/); [Bain insight](https://www.bain.com/insights/e-conomy-sea-2025/).
- Regulation: Vietnam's Law 122/2025/QH15 on E-commerce was adopted 10 Dec 2025 and takes effect 1 Jul 2026. It is the first law to explicitly regulate livestream selling. Hosts must not give false or misleading information about uses, origin, quality or price, and platforms must verify host identity — [B-Company](https://b-company.jp/vietnams-new-e-commerce-law-122-2025-qh15-key-provisions-and-implications/); [Vietnam News](https://vietnamnews.vn/economy/1725726/viet-nam-s-upcoming-e-commerce-law-to-tighten-rules-on-livestream-sellers.html); [Vietstock Jan 2026](https://en.vietstock.vn/2026/01/regulations-for-livestream-sales-tightened-38-628425.htm). China's livestream e-commerce supervision measures (Order No. 117) require real-time monitoring and 3-year record retention, and ban AI-fabricated misleading information — [Digital Policy Alert](https://digitalpolicyalert.org/event/36842-supervision-and-management-measures-for-live-streaming-e-commerce-order-no-117-including-content-moderation-regulation-were-adopted); [China Briefing](https://www.china-briefing.com/news/china-livestream-advertising-regulations-compliance/).
- Platform rule: TikTok Shop requires *real-time human* speech, with no AI voices or recordings. Violations bring commission restrictions, removal and bans through the Creator Health Rating — [PYMNTS](https://www.pymnts.com/?p=3823468); [NetInfluencer](https://www.netinfluencer.com/tiktok-shop-bans-ai-voices-prerecorded-audio-from-promotional-livestreams/). Compliance guidance recommends "approved claims libraries, restricted language lists… real-time monitoring" — [Influencers-Time](https://www.influencers-time.com/tiktok-shop-supplement-livestream-compliance-framework/).
- Existing tools fall short: AnyLive *replaces* hosts with avatars, which TikTok now bans — [AnyMind](https://anymindgroup.com/news/press-release/ai_influencers_live_commerce). TikTok's Seller Assistant is a back-office chatbot, not a live-audio monitor — [GDI](https://www.globaldatinginsights.com/featured/tiktok-shop-rolls-out-new-tools-to-boost-seller-efficiency/). Platform moderation enforces *after* the fact. I found no seller-side tool that whispers "don't say 'cures acne' — not BPOM-registered" to the host in real time.

**Gap 2 — Halal / BPOM / multi-market label compliance checker from packaging photos for small online sellers (Indonesia first, then ASEAN)**
- Regulation: BPJPH Circular Letter No. 7/2025 (28 Aug 2025) requires certified businesses to (i) display the national halal label in the official design and placement and (ii) publish halal status across digital channels: marketplaces, websites and social media, including tagging @halal.indonesia. **The old MUI logo transition ends 17 Oct 2026** — [SSEK](https://ssek.com/blog/indonesia-halal-certification-compliance-strengthened-mandatory-labelling-and-publication-for-certified-products/); [Mondaq](https://www.mondaq.com/dodd-frank-consumer-protection-act/1703166/indonesia-halal-certification-compliance-strengthened-mandatory-labelling-and-publication-for-certified-products). Beauty products sold online need BPOM permission — [Republika](https://en.republika.co.id/berita/s5k6ol490).
- Cross-border friction: more than 80% of 700 MSMEs across six ASEAN economies report cross-border e-commerce challenges — [TechRepublic](https://www.techrepublic.com/article/news-asean-cross-border-ecommerce-apac/). Product certification differs by country (Singapore safety marks, Thailand TISI, Malaysia SIRIM). Labelling also differs ("best before" vs "expiry", front-of-pack, nutrition panels), causing re-labelling costs that "fall most heavily on MSMEs" — [Alibaba Seller Blog 2026](https://seller.alibaba.com/blogs/2026/southeast-asia/small-appliances/compliance-driven-market-reshuffle); [ADB/ASEAN-BAC](https://aric.adb.org/pdf/events/attn/202510/Session%203.3_ASEAN-BAC%20-%20Private%20Sector%20Perspective%20on%20Cooperation_Rifiki.pdf).
- Existing tools: none found that check a *photo of a pack plus a marketplace listing* against ID/MY/TH/VN rules and output fixes. Compliance today is done by consultants and law firms (inference from the sources above being law-firm advisories).

**Gap 3 — Shopkeeper-side "store brain" for kirana/sari-sari/warung: shelf photo → stock gaps → distributor reorder + credit-risk-aware khata**
- Need: India has about 13M kiranas. Their share fell from 95% (2018) to 92.6% (2023) and is projected at 88.9% by 2028. 46% of quick-commerce users cut kirana purchases (Kearney) — [Storyboard18](https://www.storyboard18.com/amp/how-it-works/quick-commerce-disrupts-indias-grocery-industry-kirana-stores-hit-hard-kearney-68991.htm); [Indian Retailer](https://www.indianretailer.com/news/kirana-stores-india-turn-digitalisation-amid-rising-quick-commerce-competition). About 2 lakh kiranas reportedly closed — [Outlook Business](https://www.outlookbusiness.com/news/nearly-2-lakh-kirana-stores-close-as-battle-for-survival-against-quick-commerce-players-intensifies). 80% of kiranas say they need digitalisation to win back business from Zepto and Blinkit — [CXOToday](https://cxotoday.com/media-coverage/80-kirana-stores-express-need-for-digitalisation-win-back-business-from-zepto-blinkit/).
- Existing tools fall short: shelf recognition serves *brands* (ShelfWatch, Trax), not the shopkeeper's reorder decision — [ParallelDots](https://paralleldots.com); [IndiaRetailing](https://www.indiaretailing.com/?p=48632). Ledgers (OkCredit) and B2B ordering (Flipkart/Sarvam) are separate apps — [OkCredit](https://okcredit.in/en/); [MIT Sloan ME](https://www.mitsloanme.com/article/flipkart-brings-voice-led-wholesale-ordering-to-whatsapp-with-sarvam-ai/). Packworks bundles inventory, insights and loans in the Philippines, which shows demand but also narrows novelty there — [Inquirer](https://plus.inquirer.net/business/ai-tool-boosts-sari-sari-store-sales/).

**Gap 4 — Near-expiry rescue for small grocers (expiry-date OCR from photos → markdown/bundle → neighbourhood WhatsApp offer)**
- Need: Indonesia generates an estimated 5-19M tons of food waste a year at retail and consumption stages. Grocery-store waste is estimated at IDR 40-50M per month per store, mostly fresh items, and stores use ad-hoc discounting up to 50% — [Prasetiya Mulya abstract](https://elib.prasetiyamulya.ac.id/999/5/05.%20ABSTRAK.pdf); [KADIN](https://kadin.id/en/analisa/miliaran-terbuang-mengatasi-krisis-sisa-pangan-di-indonesia/); [FAO AGRIS](https://agris.fao.org/search/en/records/67597dc0c7a957febdf8decc). Policy context: [UN-PAGE West Java FLW](https://www.un-page.org/knowledge-hub/policy-assessment-on-food-loss-waste-in-west-java-indonesia).
- Evidence quality: the per-store figure comes from one academic study and may describe a grocery *chain*, not micro-stores. I found no 2025 data specific to small grocers.

**Gap 5 — Blind/low-vision voice shopping agent for Indian e-commerce (Indic languages)**
- Need: India's Supreme Court (Pragya Prasun v. Union of India, 30 Apr 2025) held that digital access is part of Article 21. It ordered WCAG compliance, accessibility audits and blind users in UAT (the ruling concerned digital KYC) — [AZB Partners](https://www.azbpartners.com/bank/bridging-the-digital-divide-indias-evolving-accessibility-framework/); [MediaNama](https://www.medianama.com/2025/05/223-digital-access-life-liberty-supreme-court-ruling/); [Business Today](https://www.businesstoday.in/amp/personal-finance/news/story/supreme-court-mandates-inclusive-ekyc-for-disabled-says-digital-access-a-right-report-474226-2025-04-30). Research finds Indian e-commerce sites are not accessible to blind users even when nominally WCAG-conformant — [IOS Press, Technology & Disability](https://content.iospress.com/articles/technology-and-disability/tad200293). That paper is pre-2024, which weakens the evidence.

**Gap 6 — Multilingual "AI shop clerk" for understaffed small shops serving inbound tourists in Japan**
- Need: Japanese retail faces labor-shortage bankruptcies, shortened hours and declining service even while inbound tourism booms — [Stripe JP](https://stripe.com/jp/resources/more/challenges-in-japanese-retail-industry). This is a weak, vendor-authored source. I did not get primary data (JNTO arrivals, Teikoku Databank labor-shortage bankruptcies).

### Inferences
- **Gap 1 is the top pick for the judging weights.** Gemini Live API takes the streamed audio/video of the host plus the chat feed. A RAG claims library (Vietnam Law 122, BPOM/halal, TikTok policy) returns grounded violations with cited rule text. A teleprompter suggests compliant answers to viewer questions, which the human speaks, so it fits TikTok's real-time rule. An audit log is kept, mirroring China's 3-year retention model. Cloud Run handles the websocket and Firebase the host UI. This scores on Technical/GenAI (real-time multimodal, grounding, function calling), Impact (more than 3M video sellers) and Innovation (assist-the-human, not an avatar).
- **Gap 2 is the most timely.** The demo would be: photo of a pack plus a Tokopedia/Shopee listing URL. Gemini vision extracts the label, checks the halal logo version (old MUI vs new BPJPH) and BPOM number format, and flags missing digital halal publication. It then generates corrected listing text in Bahasa, Malay, Thai and Vietnamese. The 17 Oct 2026 deadline is a compelling pitch hook, and Gemma could run on-device for offline sellers. Rule correctness needs careful sourcing, so scope it to 1-2 categories (food, cosmetics).
- **Gap 3 is buildable but less novel.** Differentiate on *agentic reconciliation*: shelf photo (SKU110K-style) → gap list → price comparison across a distributor's WhatsApp price list (image/PDF) → draft order in local language. Add a khata-aware "who to extend credit to" view. Avoid pitching it as "voice khata" or "shelf audit".
- Gaps 4-6 have thinner evidence. Gap 4 combines well with Gap 3 as one feature. Gap 5 is high-impact for UX/social scoring, but agent-driven browsing of third-party marketplaces raises ToS issues. Gap 6 overlaps with generic translation apps.
- **Problem alignment:** Gaps 1 and 2 map to "conversational commerce" and "intelligent business experiences". Gap 3 maps to "inventory intelligence" and "demand forecasting" in the suggested directions.

### Gaps
- I did not find Indonesian or Thai livestream-specific laws comparable to Vietnam Law 122 (searched, not found). I also have no figures on fines or enforcement counts for misleading livestream claims in SEA.
- No market data was found on how many Indonesian online sellers are not yet halal-label compliant ahead of 17 Oct 2026.
- Novelty is claimed as "no product found in these searches", not as a full patent or market scan. Teams should re-check with targeted searches (e.g. "livestream compliance copilot", "halal label checker AI") before committing.
- I did not fetch Vietnam Law 122 penalty provisions or the full text of China's Order 117.
- Japan, Korea and ANZ retail gaps are under-researched here (one weak source).
