# Sustainability & Social Impact — JAPAC whitespace gaps for Google Cloud AI Builder Cup 2026

Scope: unsolved JAPAC gaps (2024–2026 evidence) that a 2–4 person team could build in about 10 days with Gemini/Gemma, Earth Engine, BigQuery, and Cloud Run. The notes also cover crowded areas to avoid. Research date: 2026-10-07.

## Q1. Which areas are saturated (the "crowded — avoid" list)?

### Takeaway
Big players (Google, Microsoft/AI4Bharat, Digital Green, carbon-project developers, GMW) already cover riverine flood forecasting, satellite building-damage detection, vernacular agri-advisory bots, vernacular welfare-scheme chatbots, smallholder carbon project development, and global mangrove extent mapping. A hackathon entry that copies any of these will score low on Innovation. The open space is the last mile *around* these platforms: turning their outputs into actions for specific vulnerable users and local officials.

### Cited Findings
- **Flood forecasting (avoid):** Google Flood Hub now covers 80 countries and 460M people, with forecasts up to 7 days ahead, and includes Asia-Pacific. It combines a hydrologic model with an inundation model — [Google blog](https://blog.google/outreach-initiatives/sustainability/flood-hub-ai-flood-forecasting-more-countries/); [Android Central](https://www.androidcentral.com/apps-software/google-flood-hub-expanding-to-80-countries). *Remaining hole:* Flood Hub covers only riverine floods, so flash, urban, and coastal floods get less warning — [Android Central](https://www.androidcentral.com/apps-software/google-flood-hub-expanding-to-80-countries).
- **Satellite building-damage AI (avoid as a core model):** Google Research's SKAI combines Open Buildings with SKAI Zero-Shot and Fine-Tuned damage models. It was open-sourced with WFP and is used with UN partners — [UN Global Pulse](https://www.unglobalpulse.org/ai-from-google-research-and-un-boosts-humanitarian-disaster-response-wider-coverage-faster-damage-assessments). xBD (850,736 building annotations across 45,362 km²) is the standard benchmark, and many models have already been trained on it — [arXiv xBD](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1911.09296).
- **Agri-advisory bots (avoid):** Digital Green's Farmer.Chat gives multilingual voice, text, and image advice on pests, seeds, fertilizer, weather, and markets. FY25 figures: 165k farmers across India, Ethiopia, Kenya, Nigeria and Brazil, 2M questions, 70% of interactions in local languages — [Digital Green FY25 Annual Report](https://digitalgreen.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/Digital-Green-FY25-Annual-Report.pdf). Another source reports more than 1M users — [WSA Global](https://wsa-global.org/winner/farmerchat/). (The two user counts conflict; they probably measure different things at different dates.)
- **Vernacular welfare-scheme chatbots (avoid the generic version):** Jugalbandi (AI4Bharat with Microsoft Azure OpenAI) runs over WhatsApp. It answers eligibility and documentation questions in 10 of India's 22 official languages and covers 171 of about 20,000 government programs — [Microsoft Source Asia](https://news.microsoft.com/source/asia/features/with-help-from-next-generation-ai-indian-villagers-gain-easier-access-to-government-services/).
- **Smallholder carbon project development (avoid competing head-on):** Varaha raised $8.7M to run full-stack carbon MRV and credit sales for smallholders in Asia and Africa — [AgFunderNews](https://agfundernews.com/varaha-raises-8-7m-to-help-smallholders-in-asia-africa-transition-to-climate-friendly-farming-methods). Boomitra's AI and satellite MRV lets plots as small as one acre take part. Regrow, Boomitra and CarbonFarm presented rice MRV methods at an IRRI Bangkok workshop — [IRRI](https://www.irri.org/news-and-events/news/mobilizing-investment-resilient-and-low-emission-agrifood-systems-asia-through).
- **Global mangrove extent mapping (avoid):** Global Mangrove Watch already provides near-real-time remote-sensing change data for mangroves worldwide, including Indonesia — [Wetlands International](https://www.wetlands.org/how-global-mangrove-watch-empowers-local-action-in-indonesia/).
- **Government heat-index maps (avoid duplicating):** In April 2025 PAGASA launched iHeatMap, which gives gridded, hourly, colour-coded heat-index forecasts. Its interface is "optimized for desktop browsing" — [PAGASA press release](https://bagong.pagasa.dost.gov.ph/press-release/174?page=4).
- **Social-media damage triage (well studied):** AI damage assessment from citizen social-media images is an established research line (AAAI 2022) — [AAAI](https://ojs.aaai.org/index.php/AAAI/article/view/21521).
- **Arsenic well screening:** iArsenic, a social-networking arsenic-screening tool for Bangladeshi wells, already exists — [Univ. of Portsmouth](https://researchportal.port.ac.uk/en/projects/iarsenic-instant-arsenic-screening-of-groundwater-wells-in-bangla/).

### Inferences
- Carbon footprint calculators, Plantix-style crop-disease photo apps, waste-sorting camera apps, generic disaster chatbots, wildfire detection, and ESG reporting SaaS (Persefoni, Watershed, Sweep) are widely known as crowded. They should stay on the avoid list. However, I did not collect 2024–26 citations for each of them in this session (see Gaps).
- The pattern that sets an entry apart: use the big platforms (Flood Hub, SKAI/Open Buildings, PAGASA iHeatMap, GMW) as *inputs*. Then use Gemini for what they lack — multimodal field evidence, local-language and accessible output, and filling in official forms or workflows.

### Gaps
- No 2024–26 primary-source coverage numbers were collected for Plantix, Kissan AI, wildfire detection, carbon calculators, waste-sorting apps, or ESG SaaS. Their place on the avoid list comes from the brief, not from verified evidence in this session.

## Q2. Candidate whitespace gaps (ranked), with evidence, existing tools, buildability, and demo data

### Takeaway
The strongest candidates are (1) a citizen-photo-to-RDANA damage and needs tool for Philippine LGUs, (2) prevention of "disaster-related deaths" among elderly and foreign evacuees in Japan, (3) heat-stress work-rest coaching for informal outdoor workers, and (4) disability-inclusive early-warning translation. Candidates (5) cooperative-level rice AWD evidence, (6) community mangrove-restoration survival monitoring, and (7) waste-picker traceability receipts are viable but differentiate less or have weaker evidence.

### Cited Findings

**Gap 1 — Citizen and barangay photos → auto-filled RDANA reports for LGUs (Philippines)**
- Scale: Typhoon Kalmaegi (Nov 2025) left 250 dead, 5.18M people affected and 110.3K houses damaged. Fung-wong followed within days, affecting more than 4.67M people and displacing about 955K — [AHA Centre weekly update](https://ahacentre.org/weekly-disaster-update/weekly-disaster-update-3-9-november-2025/); [ReliefWeb/ASEAN wk46](https://reliefweb.int/report/philippines/asean-weekly-disaster-update-week-46-10-16-november-2025).
- Mandate: RA 10121 requires LDRRMCs to institutionalize RDANA, which covers damage, secondary threats, critical needs, and local capacity. RDANA Form 2 is due within 3 weeks. LGUs are still running manual RDANA trainings in 2025 — [Hinigaran/LGU page](https://hingyon.gov.ph/rdana-training-strengthens-local-disaster-response-capacity/); [LRTA 2025 training](https://www.lrta.gov.ph/rapid-damage-assessment-and-needs-analysis-rdana-training-august-19-22-2025/).
- Shortfall of existing tools: SKAI works on satellite or aerial imagery at building level — [UN Global Pulse](https://www.unglobalpulse.org/ai-from-google-research-and-un-boosts-humanitarian-disaster-response-wider-coverage-faster-damage-assessments). It does not capture ground-level needs (water, shelter, medical) or produce the official RDANA forms. Expert survey methods delay response — [AAAI 2022](https://ojs.aaai.org/index.php/AAAI/article/view/21521).
- Build: field responders and residents submit Tagalog, Cebuano or English voice notes plus geotagged photos. Gemini multimodal classifies the damage and extracts needs into the RDANA schema. The tool cross-checks with Open Buildings and Sentinel-1/2 change detection in Earth Engine, stores records in BigQuery, and outputs a pre-filled RDANA PDF and a barangay needs dashboard on Cloud Run. Pub/Sub handles intake.
- Demo data: xBD — [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1911.09296); ReliefWeb and AHA Centre situation reports for Kalmaegi and Fung-wong (links above).

**Gap 2 — Preventing "disaster-related deaths" for elderly and foreign residents after a disaster (Japan, and other ageing societies)**
- Noto (Jan 2024): 228 direct deaths plus 397 disaster-related deaths as of July 2025. More than 90% of the disaster-related deaths were people aged 70+. Causes included long shelter stays, power and water outages, and breakdowns in welfare and medical care — [nippon.com](https://www.nippon.com/en/in-depth/d01152/failures-of-noto-disaster-relief-drive-major-reforms-japan%E2%80%99s-revised-laws-target-infrast.html); [Vietnam News/Kyodo](https://vietnamnews.vn/world/1690908/japan-sees-5-400-deaths-from-post-disaster-fatigue-stress-since-1995.html). About 18,000 people were still in temporary housing two years on — [Tuoi Tre](https://news.tuoitre.vn/18000-still-in-temporary-housing-2-years-after-noto-quake-103251231161104918.htm).
- Foreign residents, including Southeast Asian technical interns, were reluctant to seek help and avoided shelters. More than 60% cited language barriers and lack of information — [PMC study](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC12095853/); [nippon.com/Jiji](https://nippon.com/en/news/yjj2024122500546). The response so far has been interpreter drills and promoting an existing multilingual alert app. That covers alerts, not ongoing post-disaster welfare — [nippon.com/Jiji](https://nippon.com/en/news/yjj2024122500546).
- Build: a shelter and home-evacuee check-in agent using Gemini Live voice in Japanese dialects, Vietnamese, Indonesian, Tagalog, and Nepali. It runs a daily health and needs triage (meds, DVT risk, dehydration, isolation) and escalates to welfare staff through a BigQuery risk queue. Gemma can run on-device when the network is down.
- Demo data: synthetic evacuee profiles; JMA/J-ALERT feed formats; shelter locations from OpenStreetMap.

**Gap 3 — Heat-stress work-rest coaching for informal outdoor workers (South and Southeast Asia)**
- ESCAP Asia-Pacific Disaster Report 2025 (*Rising Heat, Rising Risk*): heat is the region's fastest-growing climate hazard. A 2024 heatwave affected about 33M people in Bangladesh, and one in India caused about 700 deaths. Urban heat islands add 2–7°C in Manila, Dhaka, Delhi and other cities. ESCAP plans space-based heat early warning and inclusive social protection — [UNESCAP](https://www.unescap.org/news/un-warns-rising-heat-risks-asia-and-pacific); [PreventionWeb](https://www.preventionweb.net/quick/98721).
- ILO (July 2024): 74.7% of Asia-Pacific workers are exposed to excessive heat, against a 71% global average. Eight in ten occupational heat injuries happen *outside* declared heatwaves — [ILO](https://www.ilo.org/resource/news/more-workers-ever-are-losing-fight-against-heat-stress).
- Informal workers (street vendors, construction labourers, delivery riders) are the most exposed and least protected. Heat may cost them about 2% of GDP, nearly $80B. Some report that 8-hour tasks now take 10–12 hours — [Dhaka Tribune](https://www.dhakatribune.com/world/asia/416723/extreme-heat-costs-asia%E2%80%99s-poorest-workers-health); [IIED](https://www.iied.org/too-hot-work-too-poor-rest). Gig riders lack labour protections, and an RCT with 276 Delhi gig workers is planned for 2025 — [Social Science Registry](https://www.socialscienceregistry.org/versions/293658/docs/version/document).
- Shortfall: PAGASA iHeatMap is a desktop map, not a worker-level coach — [PAGASA](https://bagong.pagasa.dost.gov.ph/press-release/174?page=4).
- Build: a WhatsApp or PWA voice agent in Tagalog, Bangla or Hindi. It takes the worker's job type, location and shift, plus a photo of the worksite (shade, exertion). It combines the heat-index forecast with Earth Engine land-surface temperature (MODIS/Landsat) to give a personal work-rest-hydrate schedule, and finds the nearest cooling or water points from OSM. Anonymised aggregates go to LGUs and cooperatives in BigQuery.
- Demo data: ERA5 / ERA5-Land, MODIS LST and Landsat in Earth Engine; the PAGASA heat index; OSM.

**Gap 4 — Disability-inclusive early-warning translation**
- People with disabilities die at 2–4 times the rate of others in many disasters, partly because warnings are not adapted. Audio-only signals fail deaf people, who then depend on neighbours — [UN DESA](https://www.un.org/en/development/desa/news/social/inclusion-saves-lives.html); [Pacific Disability Forum note](https://pacificdisability.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/01/DiDRR_Background-note.pdf). Pacific Sendai reviews still list inclusive early warning as an open priority to 2030 — [PreventionWeb](https://preventionweb.net/quick/43852).
- Build: Gemini turns official CAP alerts (PAGASA, BMKG, JMA, NDMA) into easy-read text, pictogram cards, local-language TTS, haptic patterns, and simple per-person action plans keyed to a household's disability profile. A barangay or *chonaikai* (neighbourhood association) helper roster flags who must be checked on in person.
- Demo data: public CAP feeds; Flood Hub forecasts as the trigger.

**Gap 5 — Cooperative-owned evidence of alternate wetting and drying (AWD) for rice smallholders**
- Rice produces about 10% of global methane, more than a third of it from Southeast Asia. MRV for a 3,060 ha Vietnamese project costs more than USD 325,000 to set up plus USD 55,000 a year, while credits sell for about USD 7/tCO2e. IRRI (Apr 2026) calls this a "cost trap" and urges Tier 2 MRV — [IRRI Rice Today](https://ricetoday.irri.org/why-mrv-is-the-missing-link-for-low-emission-rice-in-southeast-asia/); [AgNavigator](https://www.agnavigator.com/Article/2026/04/21/mrv-systems-the-missing-link-in-scaling-low-emission-rice-irri-report/).
- Build: Sentinel-1 SAR flood and dry-cycle detection per field in Earth Engine, plus farmer field-tube photos and voice logs. Gemini checks consistency and writes an audit-ready evidence dossier for the cooperative. That dossier can feed a low-carbon rice certification premium or a buyer, without requiring a full carbon-credit developer.
- Risk: neighbours Varaha and Boomitra — [AgFunderNews](https://agfundernews.com/varaha-raises-8-7m-to-help-smallholders-in-asia-africa-transition-to-climate-friendly-farming-methods). Position it as a cheap pre-MRV tool or a tool for premium certification.

**Gap 6 — Survival monitoring for community mangrove restoration**
- In 47 Sumatran restoration projects, seedling survival ranged from 0% to 99%. Practitioners recommend standard survival plots, salinity and pH readings, and time-lapse photos — [Conservation Evidence](https://conservationevidencejournal.com/individual-study/9213); [Planète Urgence 2026](https://planete-urgence.org/en/dans-le-delta-de-la-mahakam-restaurer-les-mangroves-avec-les-communautes-locales/). Indonesia holds more than 20% of the world's mangroves. GMW tracks extent, not plot-level survival — [Wetlands International](https://www.wetlands.org/how-global-mangrove-watch-empowers-local-action-in-indonesia/).
- Build: Gemini vision counts and assesses seedlings from fixed-point plot photos, cross-checks with Sentinel-2 NDVI and GMW, and writes donor reports in Bahasa and English automatically.

**Gap 7 — Traceability receipts for informal waste pickers' sales**
- The informal sector's recycling contribution is "invisible" and material origin is untraceable. Pilots in Indonesia and India use POS devices to register pickers as suppliers. South Asia shows a "policy–practice gap" on EPR (extended producer responsibility) — [Center for Data Innovation](https://datainnovation.org/2022/01/5-qs-for-siddharth-hande-founder-and-ceo-of-kabadiwalla-connect/); [IGES 2025 session](https://www.iges.or.jp/sites/default/files/2025-11/Key%20Takeaways%20from%20the%20Session-%20R1.pdf).
- Build: a picker photographs a sack. Gemini estimates material grade and weight band, issues a signed digital receipt, and builds EPR-ready provenance in BigQuery for brands.
- Weaker differentiation: Kabadiwalla Connect and POS pilots already exist.

### Inferences
- Best fit with the judging criteria (40% technical and GenAI): Gaps 1 and 3 combine Gemini multimodal input, Earth Engine, BigQuery and an agentic output (forms and schedules), so they show the most stack depth. Gap 2 has the most emotionally compelling evidence (a 90%+ elderly share of disaster-related deaths) and a clear "nobody does this" story.
- Gap 1 has the best demo data: real Kalmaegi situation reports, xBD, and Open Buildings.
- Each candidate gets its novelty from linking existing platform outputs to a specific vulnerable user plus a specific official workflow, such as the RDANA form or a welfare check-in.

### Gaps
- I found no source showing that any product auto-fills RDANA forms from citizen photos. That absence suggests whitespace, but I did not confirm it with an exhaustive product search.
- I did not research NGO grant and impact reporting for small NGOs, elder isolation in Korea and Singapore, or 2024–26 data on arsenic and groundwater. The only arsenic sources found were pre-2015.
- I did not verify current coverage of Japan's multilingual disaster apps (e.g., JNTO Safety Tips) or check whether they support post-disaster welfare check-ins.
